# Exercise workbooks (`model/exercises/`)

Every workbook here is macro-free (.xlsx, no VBA part, iterative calculation off) and uses live formulas, with inputs in blue on pale yellow and checks that show 0 when passing. Each was built by the script named in its row and verified by `verify_exercises.py`: LibreOffice 24.2 headless recalculation under a private profile (`-env:UserInstallation=file:///tmp/lo_profile_buildalong`), then comparison with Python computations of the answers stated in the briefs. Built and verified October 3, 2026, against Case P model version 1.4 and the round-2 u09 brief.

Search basis: every `model/exercises/` reference and every "supplied" workbook in `bible/briefs/u*.md` (u03 Chapter 13; u09 Chapters 39, 43, 44 and Section 0.7 R8; u17 Chapters 85 and 86). No other brief names a supplied or answer workbook.

## Interpretations used where a brief is silent

- **Exercise 43.17** (all reproduce every stated answer): construction cash flows at month ends; contingency in proportion to the February to December PV EPC percentages; IDC at 7.25%/12 and the commitment fee at 0.70%/12 on the undrawn commitment at the start of each month; energy of operating year y = 512.8 × 0.996^(y−1), compounding; prices and fixed opex step up each January 1; tax on revenue less opex, interest and depreciation, nil in 2028 to 2030 with holiday losses lost; depreciation over 40 half-years on total uses less the DSRA; DSRA balance at each half-year end equal to the next half-year's debt service (no interest earned), released at 2042H2; historic DSCR over this and the previous half-year; locked cash released when the test next passes; the debt locked at the sizing amount and profile in the base and downside cases.
- **Chapter 13 practice workbook:** the two timelines of Example 13.2 sit on separate row blocks of the Time sheet (monthly rows 4 to 12, semiannual rows 18 to 25), so the semiannual rows are not at the "row 16" the walkthrough lists for a single timeline; Example 13.3's delay months are entered by their stated lengths (31, 30, 31, 31, 30, 31, 30, 31 days) because the brief gives no dates for the delay; operations end 20 years after COD (April 30, 2049), the operating life of Examples 13.5 and 13.6; rates are Excel percentages, as Chapter 13's own formulas use them.
- **Exercise 85.12:** the Equation 85.2 block sits in column G with the brief's row numbers (the brief's column-C form would overlap the Equation 85.1 block on one tab); verdict cells state the binding test and the breakeven declines without adding thresholds the brief does not give.
