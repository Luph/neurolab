# Case T reference model: report

Merrick Link toll road PPP (Brannock, Commonwealth of Ardmore). Model version 1.0, story as of October 3, 2026. All amounts ARD millions (nominal) unless stated. Generated by `model/case_t.py`; every number below is computed, none is typed by hand.

## 1. Model structure and conventions

- Timeline: 16 construction columns (stub 2015-05-27 to 2015-06-30, then quarters to 2019-03-31), then a 2019-03-31 to 2019-06-30 column and semiannual periods to 2059-06-30 (97 columns; first model period in Excel column J).
- Scenarios: 1 bid base (Pellow), 2 banking (Ridgeway, live sizing), 3 downside, 4 actual history, 5 restructuring case (Ridgeway 2023), 6 retender valuation at June 30, 2022, 7 to 13 sensitivities on the bid base.
- Financing is sized live on the banking case (scenario 2) and locked for all other runs; the Restructured Senior Notes and restructured NILO profile are sculpted live in scenario 5 and locked for scenario 4.
- Circularity: Gauss-Seidel iteration of the whole model to a tolerance of 1e-09 (ARD million) on every tracked series and scalar. Banking run converged in 15 passes. The workbook uses iterative calculation (100 iterations, 0.000001 maximum change) with a circuit breaker `Circ` on the Inputs sheet: Circ = 0 cuts the financing-cost and sizing feedback so errors flush out; set it back to 1 to recalculate.
- Day counts: bank, bridge, NILO, escrow and shareholder loans ACT/365; bonds and notes 30/360.
- Sign convention: costs stored positive and subtracted; equity cash flows negative for contributions.
