How to read and maintain it:

1. Part 1 reproduces Exhibit FM.1 with the sixth column, sub-skills, that appears only in this file (u17 00-3). Part 2 gives, for each capability, every chapter whose binding can-do list or capability table cites it, and the worked examples, walkthroughs and exercises the briefs name, with the capstone task and the examination allocation.
2. Every chapter's "What you will be able to do" list is binding (capabilities review defect 11). Phase 6 regenerates this file mechanically from the final chapters' can-do lists and exercise labels and diffs it against Part 1; the Phase 4 consistency checker confirms that every item marked here exists in the drafted chapter.
3. Item labels are the briefs' round 1 labels. Where a brief renumbers (u11 Exercise 51.13 inserted, old 51.13 to 51.16 now 51.14 to 51.17; u13 Exercises 62.16, 63.16, 64.19, 64.20 placed out of numeric order; u15 and u16 example relabels), the drafted chapter's labels govern and this file is updated.
4. A dash means the brief names no item of that kind for that capability; it does not mean the chapter has none. Every chapter's judgment drill serves Capability 15.
