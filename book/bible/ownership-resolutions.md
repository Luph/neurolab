# Ownership resolutions

Status: binding, 2026-10-03. Author: architecture editor, for the editor-in-chief. Rulings take precedence over the unit briefs (`bible/briefs/u01.md` to `u17.md`) and over `bible/brief-feedback.md`. Where a ruling changes a brief, the writer of that chapter applies the change when drafting; the brief files are not rewritten. Derived files: `bible/glossary-canon.md` (one home per term), `bible/anchor-registry.md` (labels), `bible/architecture.md` (titles and Part XVI Owns notes), the style sheet addendum of 2026-10-03, and decisions D-015 onward.

Governing rule (standards Section 11, Phase 1 item 2): every concept has exactly one home chapter where it is taught in full; other chapters cross-reference the home section and never re-teach it; no chapter relies on a concept taught later except through a one- or two-sentence explicit forward reference. Where two briefs claimed the same concept, the home is the earliest chapter that must teach it substantively, unless the architecture assigns it elsewhere and the earlier chapter can work with a forward reference.

Each ruling states: the concept; its home (chapter, section label); what other chapters do (cross-reference, one-line preview, or forward reference); and the brief text that must change. "CR" codes refer to the unit issue lists at the end of each brief; "self-detected" marks conflicts found in this pass by comparing concepts-owned lists, tables of contents, glossaries and anchors.
