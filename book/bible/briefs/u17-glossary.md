# Unit u17 glossary (Chapters 85 to 88)

Terms owned by Chapters 85 to 88, with canonical definitions and home sections. The matter files (00, 89 to 94) own no glossary terms. Ownership questions are logged in `u17.md` under CR-u17-05 and CR-u17-06.

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition | Home section |
|---|---|---|---|
| deal screen | – | A time-boxed first assessment of an opportunity that decides whether to spend the next tranche of diligence time and money, and on what conditions. | 85.1.1 |
| one-hour deal screen | – | This book's six-block framework for screening any project in an hour and reaching a proceed, proceed-with-conditions or decline verdict. | 85.2 |
| fatal flaw | – | A risk that no price, structure or condition can make bankable within the deal's constraints. | 85.2.4 |
| screen note | – | The one-page record of a deal screen: the deal summary, the deciding risks ranked, the numbers with their errors, the verdict, conditions and information requests. | 85.2.6 |
| teaser | – | A short, non-confidential description of an opportunity circulated to gauge interest before a confidentiality agreement is signed. | 85.8 |
| annuity shortcut | – | Screening debt capacity as the level debt service CFADS supports at the target DSCR, discounted as an annuity at the all-in rate over the tenor. | 85.3.2 |
| affordability ratio | – | An offtaker's fixed payment commitments to IPPs, including a proposed project, as a share of its revenue; a screening flag, not a market test. | 85.3.5 |
| virtual data room | VDR | A secure online repository in which a seller, sponsor or borrower makes documents available to bidders, lenders and advisors under access controls and logs. | 85.5.1 |
| Q&A log | – | The data room's record of questions asked and answers given, which in a competitive process is shared with all bidders and forms part of the disclosure. | 85.5.1 |
| clean team | – | A restricted group, usually of advisors or ring-fenced staff, allowed to see competitively sensitive information on terms that stop it reaching the wider deal team. | 85.5.5 |
| number trace | – | Following one figure through every document in which it appears and reconciling each difference. | 85.5.3 |
| absence index | – | A list of the documents and facts a deal of this type should have, marked to show which are missing, late, draft or unsigned. | 85.5.4 |
| credit paper | – | The document on which a lender's credit committee decides whether to commit, underwrite or hold a facility, and against which the decision is later judged. | 86.1.1 |
| investment committee memo | IC memo | The document on which an equity investor's investment committee decides whether to invest, at what price and on what conditions. | 86.4.1 |
| recommendation and request | – | The opening of a credit paper stating the decision sought (amount, facility, role, tenor, hold), the recommendation and its reasons. | 86.2.1 |
| sponsor case | – | The projections presented by the sponsor, before the lender's or investor's own adjustments. | 86.2.5 |
| bank case | – | The lender's own base projections after its adjustments to the sponsor case, from which the banking and downside cases are run. | 86.2.5 |
| tornado chart | – | A chart of sensitivities ordered by the size of their effect on one output, used to show which assumption drives the downside. | 86.2.5 |
| return on risk-adjusted capital | RORAC | A facility's net income after funding cost, expected loss, operating cost and tax, divided by the capital the institution allocates to it. | 86.2.8 |
| condition of approval | – | A requirement that a committee attaches to its approval and that must be satisfied before the institution's commitment becomes effective or within a stated period. | 86.2.9 |
| monitoring trigger | – | A measurable event named in the approval that obliges the portfolio team to report or re-present the credit. | 86.2.9 |
| risk–mitigant–residual table | RMR table | This book's table that states for each material risk its mechanism, size, mitigant with document reference, the residual risk left and who bears it. | 86.3.1 |
| returns bridge | – | A step-by-step reconciliation of an equity return from the sponsor case to the investor's own case, one named assumption per step. | 86.4.2 |
| walk-away price | – | The highest price an investment committee authorizes before a competitive process ends, recorded in advance. | 86.4.3 |
| committee pre-mortem | – | This book's pre-submission review in which a colleague assumes the facility has defaulted and writes the most likely failure stories for the paper to answer. | 86.5.3 |
| annual review | – | The periodic credit paper that tests a facility's performance against the approved case and re-approves or re-grades it. | 86.6 |
| watch list | – | A lender's list of facilities under closer monitoring because of deterioration short of default. | 86.6 |
| engagement letter | – | The contract appointing an advisor, setting scope, deliverables, fees and their reopening triggers, reliance and liability limits. | 87.1.2 |
| advisor management cycle | – | This book's six-step cycle for scoping, briefing, challenging, reconciling, signing off and reviewing advisors. | 87.1.3 |
| issues list | – | The running record of every open point in a transaction, with owner, each party's position, next step and deadline. | 87.2.2 |
| integrity check | – | This book's set of questions for testing whether a proposed action could be defended if every party read a verbatim record of it. | 87.5.2 |
| intermediary | – | A third party who offers access to, or influence over, a decision-maker in return for payment; a principal bribery risk in procurement. | 87.5.1 |
| deal log | – | A practitioner's own record of every deal worked on, with the decisions taken, forecasts made and outcomes when known. | 87.8.2 |
| decision journal | – | A dated record of a decision, the reasons, the alternatives rejected and the forecast with its probability, written at the time. | 87.8.2 |
| calibration | – | The match between the probabilities a forecaster assigns and the frequency with which the forecast events happen. | 87.8.3 |
| Brier score | BS | The mean squared difference between forecast probabilities and outcomes (1 or 0); lower is better. | 87.8.3 |
| reference class | – | A set of comparable past cases whose outcomes are used to forecast a new case, such as a practitioner's own past closings. | 87.8.4 |
| outcome bias | – | Judging a decision by its result rather than by the information and reasoning available when it was made. | 87.8.1 |
| judgment-building plan | – | This book's 24-month program of deal logging, calibration review, reference classes, reconstructions and seat changes. | 87.8.7 |
| first-of-a-kind | FOAK | The first commercial-scale unit of a technology or structure, which carries costs and risks that later units will not. | 88.3.1 |
| nth-of-a-kind | NOAK | A unit built after enough repetitions that its cost and performance can be predicted from a record. | 88.3.1 |
| evidence gap | – | The absence of performance, price, legal or recovery data that lenders need to size and price a risk. | 88.1.2 |
| new-structure test | – | This book's seven-question framework for deciding whether a new structure is financeable now, with named support, or not yet. | 88.2 |
| first-of-a-kind financing ladder | FOAK ladder | This book's sequence of financing sources a technology climbs from grants to institutional debt as evidence accumulates. | 88.3.1 |
| cost gap | – | The difference between a new product's levelized cost and what an offtaker will pay, which someone must fund for the project to proceed. | 88.4.5 |
| adaptation finance | – | Finance for investment that reduces damage from climate change rather than emissions, which rarely generates its own revenue. | 88.4.7 |
| digital deal execution | – | Closing and administering financings through electronic signatures, virtual data rooms, automated review and digitally issued securities. | 88.4.11 |
