# Unit u14 glossary (Chapters 66, 67, 68, 84)

Terms owned by this unit, with canonical definitions and home sections. Definitions match the chapter briefs in `bible/briefs/u14.md`.

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition | Home section |
|---|---|---|---|
| control (accounting) | – | The power to direct the activities that most affect an investee's returns, combined with exposure to those returns and the ability to use the power to affect them; the basis for consolidation under IFRS 10. | 66.1.1 |
| consolidation | – | Presenting a parent and the entities it controls as one economic entity, line by line, with outside owners' shares shown as non-controlling interests. | 66.1.1 |
| non-controlling interest | NCI | The equity in a consolidated subsidiary that belongs to owners other than the parent. | 66.1.4 |
| substantive right | – | A right its holder has the practical ability to exercise over decisions about relevant activities, which can give or share control. | 66.1.2 |
| protective right | – | A right designed to protect its holder's interest without giving power over the investee, usually exercisable only in exceptional circumstances. | 66.1.2 |
| variable interest entity | VIE | Under US GAAP, an entity consolidated by the party that directs its most significant activities and absorbs its variability, regardless of voting share. | 66.1.3 |
| joint control | – | The contractually agreed sharing of control of an arrangement, existing only when decisions about relevant activities require the unanimous consent of the parties sharing control. | 66.2.1 |
| joint venture (accounting) | – | A joint arrangement in which the parties with joint control have rights to the net assets, accounted for by the equity method. | 66.2.2 |
| joint operation | – | A joint arrangement in which the parties with joint control have rights to the assets and obligations for the liabilities, each accounting for its share line by line. | 66.2.2 |
| equity method | – | Carrying an investment at cost adjusted for the investor's share of the investee's profit, other comprehensive income and distributions. | 66.2.3 |
| loss of control | – | The event in which a parent ceases to control a subsidiary, triggering derecognition, fair-value remeasurement of any retained interest and a gain or loss. | 66.3.2 |
| service concession arrangement | – | A public-to-private arrangement in which the grantor controls the services, users and price and the significant residual interest in the infrastructure; accounted for under IFRIC 12 or ASC 853. | 66.4.1 |
| financial asset model | – | The IFRIC 12 treatment in which the operator recognizes a receivable for its unconditional contractual right to cash from the grantor. | 66.4.2 |
| intangible asset model | – | The IFRIC 12 treatment in which the operator recognizes an intangible right to charge users. | 66.4.3 |
| bifurcated model | – | The IFRIC 12 treatment splitting consideration into a financial asset (guaranteed amounts) and an intangible (user charges). | 66.4.3 |
| embedded lease | – | A lease contained in a contract such as a PPA or toll, because the buyer obtains the right to control the use of an identified asset. | 66.5.1 |
| hedge accounting | – | Accounting that matches the timing of gains and losses on a hedging instrument with those on the hedged item. | 66.6.1 |
| cash flow hedge reserve | – | The component of other comprehensive income holding the effective portion of a cash flow hedge until the hedged cash flows affect profit or loss. | 66.6.2 |
| hypothetical derivative | – | A notional derivative with terms matching the hedged item, used to measure hedge effectiveness. | 66.6.2 |
| de-designation | – | Ending hedge accounting for all or part of a hedging relationship, after which fair-value changes go to profit or loss. | 66.6.3 |
| own-use exemption | – | The rule that a contract to buy or sell a non-financial item for the entity's own needs is not accounted for as a derivative. | 66.6.4 |
| expected credit loss | ECL | The probability-weighted present value of cash shortfalls on a financial asset, recognized as an allowance under IFRS 9. | 66.7.1 |
| frozen GAAP clause | – | A finance-document provision that fixes covenant calculations to the accounting standards in force at signing unless the parties agree otherwise. | 66.8.3 |
| accounting outcome map | – | This book's six-question framework for predicting how a project will appear in each owner's accounts and which clauses drive the result. | 66.8.5 |
| tax leakage | – | The share of a project's pre-tax cash that is lost to taxes on its way to the investor, across all taxing points. | 67.1.1 |
| tax leakage map | – | This book's framework for tracing each cash route from project to investor and recording every tax and credit along it. | 67.1.2 |
| holding company | – | An intermediate company that owns the project company's shares, used for treaty access, exit, pooling or protection. | 67.2.1 |
| double tax treaty | – | A treaty between two countries allocating taxing rights and limiting withholding taxes on cross-border income. | 67.2.2 |
| withholding tax | WHT | Tax that the payer deducts from a payment of interest, dividends or fees and remits to the tax authority on the recipient's behalf. | 67.3.1 |
| tax gross-up | – | A borrower's obligation to increase a payment so that the lender receives, after withholding, the amount it would have received without it. | 67.3.2 |
| principal purpose test | PPT | A treaty anti-abuse rule denying benefits where obtaining them was one of the principal purposes of an arrangement. | 67.2.3 |
| thin capitalization | – | A rule that denies deductions for interest on related-party debt above a set ratio to equity. | 67.4.2 |
| earnings-based interest limitation | – | A rule capping deductible net interest at a percentage of a tax-adjusted EBITDA measure. | 67.5.1 |
| public infrastructure exemption | PIE | The UK election that removes qualifying infrastructure companies' third-party non-recourse interest from the corporate interest restriction. | 67.5.3 |
| adjusted taxable income | ATI | The US section 163(j) earnings measure on which the 30% interest cap is computed. | 67.5.4 |
| transfer pricing | – | The pricing of transactions between related parties, which tax law requires to be at arm's length. | 67.6.1 |
| arm's-length principle | – | The rule that related parties must price transactions as unrelated parties would in comparable circumstances. | 67.6.1 |
| input VAT | – | VAT a business pays on its purchases, recoverable against output VAT or by refund. | 67.7.1 |
| tax holiday | – | A period during which a project's income is exempt from, or taxed at a reduced rate of, corporate income tax. | 67.8.1 |
| deferred depreciation | – | A rule allowing depreciation not used during a holiday to be deducted in later taxable years. | 67.8.2 |
| global minimum tax (Pillar Two) | GloBE | The OECD/G20 rules requiring in-scope groups to pay at least 15% effective tax in each jurisdiction. | 67.9.1 |
| qualified domestic minimum top-up tax | QDMTT | A host country's own top-up tax that collects Pillar Two top-up on local profits ahead of other jurisdictions. | 67.9.3 |
| income inclusion rule | IIR | The Pillar Two rule under which a parent jurisdiction collects top-up tax on low-taxed subsidiaries. | 67.9.3 |
| undertaxed profits rule | UTPR | The Pillar Two backstop rule collecting top-up not collected under a QDMTT or IIR. | 67.9.3 |
| substance-based income exclusion | SBIE | The Pillar Two carve-out of a percentage of tangible asset value and payroll from income subject to top-up. | 67.9.2 |
| Side-by-Side Package | SbS | The January 5, 2026 Inclusive Framework package of safe harbours, including the exemption of groups with a parent in a qualified jurisdiction from the IIR and UTPR. | 67.9.4 |
| prohibited foreign entity | PFE | Under US law after July 4, 2025, a specified foreign entity or foreign-influenced entity denied clean-electricity credits. | 67.10.2 |
| material assistance cost ratio | MACR | The share of a US facility's manufactured-product costs not attributable to prohibited foreign entities, tested against a threshold by construction-start year. | 67.10.2 |
| indirect transfer tax | – | A host-country tax on gains from an offshore sale of shares that transfers value in local assets. | 67.11.2 |
| Pillar Two screen | – | This book's five-step test of whether a host-country incentive survives the global minimum tax. | 67.9.6 |
| risk-weighted assets | RWA | Exposures multiplied by regulatory risk weights; the denominator of a bank's capital ratios. | 68.1.1 |
| regulatory capital | – | The equity and qualifying instruments a bank must hold against its risk-weighted assets. | 68.1.1 |
| standardised approach | SA | The bank capital route that assigns fixed risk weights by exposure class and external rating. | 68.2.1 |
| internal ratings-based approach | IRB | The bank capital route that uses the bank's own estimates of probability of default and, in the advanced form, loss given default. | 68.2.2 |
| probability of default | PD | The estimated likelihood that a borrower defaults within one year. | 68.2.2 |
| loss given default | LGD | The share of an exposure a lender expects to lose if the borrower defaults. | 68.2.2 |
| supervisory slotting | – | The IRB fallback for specialised lending that maps each exposure to one of five supervisory categories with fixed risk weights and expected-loss rates. | 68.2.3 |
| specialised lending | – | Lending to an entity created to finance or operate physical assets, with repayment mainly from the assets' income and substantial lender control. | 68.4.1 |
| output floor | – | The rule that a bank's modeled RWA cannot fall below a set percentage (72.5% when fully phased in) of its standardised RWA. | 68.3.2 |
| infrastructure supporting factor | – | The EU multiplier of 0.75 applied to capital requirements for qualifying infrastructure exposures. | 68.4.1 |
| high-quality project finance | – | Under EU CRR3 and Basel, an operational project exposure meeting criteria on covenants, reserves, predictable cash flows, counterparty quality, security and step-in, weighted 80%. | 68.4.1 |
| minimum margin | – | The lowest loan margin at which a lender covers expected loss, operating cost and the cost of the capital the loan consumes. | 68.5.1 |
| capital-to-price bridge | – | This book's framework for converting a project's risk weight or capital charge into a minimum margin or spread. | 68.5.2 |
| credit risk mitigation | CRM | Regulatory recognition of guarantees, collateral or credit protection that lowers the capital required on an exposure. | 68.6 |
| solvency capital requirement | SCR | The capital an EU insurer must hold to withstand a one-year 1-in-200 shock, computed by the standard formula or an internal model. | 68.7.1 |
| qualifying infrastructure investment | – | Under Solvency II, a project-level debt or equity investment meeting predictability, stress and protection criteria, earning lower capital charges. | 68.7.2 |
| matching adjustment | MA | A Solvency regime adjustment that lets insurers holding assets matched to long liabilities discount those liabilities at a higher rate. | 68.7.5 |
| highly predictable asset | HP | Under Solvency UK from June 30, 2024, an asset whose cash flows are contractually bound in time and amount, eligible for the matching adjustment within a 10% benefit limit. | 68.7.5 |
| increased costs clause | – | A facility agreement provision passing to the borrower a lender's additional cost from changes in law or regulation, including capital rules. | 68.8.3 |
| Green Bond Principles | GBP | ICMA's voluntary process guidelines for green bonds, built on use of proceeds, project evaluation and selection, management of proceeds, and reporting. | 84.2.1 |
| Green Loan Principles | GLP | The LMA, APLMA and LSTA's voluntary principles for green loans, mirroring the GBP's four core components. | 84.2.1 |
| use-of-proceeds instrument | – | A loan or bond whose label depends on applying its proceeds to eligible projects. | 84.2.1 |
| second-party opinion | SPO | An external reviewer's assessment of whether a financing framework aligns with a stated standard. | 84.2.1 |

| EU Taxonomy | – | The EU classification of environmentally sustainable activities under Regulation (EU) 2020/852. | 84.3.1 |
| do no significant harm | DNSH | The EU Taxonomy test that an activity contributing to one environmental objective does not significantly harm the others. | 84.3.1 |
