| restricted payment | – | Any payment to shareholders or their affiliates, including dividends, shareholder loan interest and principal and affiliate fees, that may be made only if the distribution conditions are met. | 51.4.5 |
| event of default | EoD | An event listed in the finance documents which, once any grace period has expired, entitles the lenders to stop funding, accelerate the debt and enforce security. | 51.5.1 |

| material adverse effect | MAE | A defined effect, usually on the project company's ability to pay or perform or on the validity of the finance documents, used to qualify representations and covenants and, in some deals, as a separate event of default. | 51.5.2 |
| grace period | – | The time after a breach during which the project company may remedy it before the breach becomes an event of default. | 51.5.2 |
| acceleration | – | The lenders' declaration, after an event of default, that all outstanding debt is immediately due. | 51.5.3 |
| drawstop | – | The lenders' right to refuse further drawdowns while a Default is continuing. | 51.5.3 |
| equity cure | – | A sponsor's right to remedy a financial covenant breach by injecting equity, counted either as additional CFADS or as a prepayment of debt, within limits on amount and frequency. | 51.5.4 |
| change of control | – | A clause requiring named sponsors to keep minimum holdings or control of the project company, breach of which is a mandatory prepayment event or an event of default. | 51.6.1 |
| sub-participation | – | A transfer of a lender's economic exposure to another party under a separate contract, without the new party becoming a lender of record. | 51.6.2 |
| majority lenders | – | Lenders whose commitments or participations exceed the threshold the facility sets for ordinary decisions, traditionally more than 66⅔% in LMA-form agreements. | 51.7.1 |
| entrenched matter | – | A decision that requires the consent of every lender, or of every affected lender, because it changes economics, maturity, security, the waterfall or the voting rules themselves. | 51.7.1 |
| defaulting lender | – | A lender that has failed to fund or has repudiated its commitment and whose votes are disregarded under the facility. | 51.7.2 |
| deemed consent | – | A clause treating a lender that does not reply to a consent request within a set period as not voting, so that its commitment is excluded from the calculation. | 51.7.2 |
| replacement of a lender | – | A borrower's right to require a lender that refuses an amendment approved by the required majority to transfer its participation at par to a willing lender. | 51.7.2 |
| waiver and amendment letter | – | The document by which lenders waive a Default or amend the finance documents on stated conditions, usually for a fee and with a reservation of rights. | 51.7.3 |

| business pledge | – | A civil-law pledge over the business as a going concern (nantissement du fonds de commerce), covering the elements the local code lists. | 52.2.2 |

| perfection | – | The steps (registration, notice, control or possession) that make a security interest effective against third parties and in insolvency. | 52.3.1 |
