## Unit-wide notes for writers

1. **Output format.** Each chapter is a LaTeX file `chapters/NN-slug.tex` (D-008). The architecture's `.md` file names map to `.tex` with the same slug. Labels follow style sheet Section 3.2. Every label listed under "Anchors" below is reserved; writers keep the numbering exactly, adding only labels for items this brief does not anticipate, and report them.
2. **Status labels.** Every example, exhibit and clause carries one of `\illustrative`, `\casep`, `\caset`, `\caser`, `\realcase{...}`. Every clause is Illustrative and drafted originally (style sheet Section 7). No LMA, LSTA, APLMA, ISDA, ICC, LCIA or ICSID model wording is reproduced or closely paraphrased; clauses are drafted from the principle.
3. **Running-case numbers.** All Case P figures come from `case-bible.md` (assumptions) or the figure ledger (IDs P-F01 to P-F36, plus the new IDs this unit requests under Case Bible flaws). Where this brief writes "from Case Bible ledger: <figure>", the writer takes the number from the ledger and never computes it. Every illustrative number below was computed and checked in Python on October 3, 2026; the key results are given so reviewers can check the chapter.
4. **Ownership boundaries inside this unit and with neighbors.** These splits are binding; each chapter cross-references rather than re-teaches.
   - Contract mechanics in general (representations, warranties, covenants, conditions, indemnities, the penalty rule, force majeure): Chapter 10. Chapter 51 teaches how those devices are built into finance documents.
   - Covenant design and levels (lock-up and default ratios, reserve sizing, sweeps, hedging requirements): Chapter 37. CFADS and ratio definitions as formulas: Chapter 35. Chapter 51 owns the drafting of covenants, distribution conditions, permitted debt and events of default; Chapter 52 owns the accounts structure and the priority of payments as documents; Chapter 42 owns the waterfall in the model.
   - Amendments, waivers and voting: Chapter 51 owns decision thresholds inside a facility or common terms agreement; Chapter 53 owns voting across creditor classes; Chapter 62 owns running a waiver in operations; Chapter 64 owns waivers in distress.
   - Security and enforcement: Chapter 52 owns the security package, the two legal traditions, perfection, enforcement as a going concern (principles); Chapter 28 owns direct agreements and step-in; Chapter 64 owns enforcement process and insolvency regimes.
   - Offshore accounts: Chapter 52 owns the account architecture as a document set; Chapter 59 owns why offshore accounts mitigate convertibility and transfer risk (see change request CR-u11-04).
   - Lenders, ECA cover, DFIs, A/B loans, preferred creditor status: Chapter 29. Islamic instruments and co-financing economics: Chapter 33. Chapter 53 owns the intercreditor rights of each class.
   - Courts versus arbitration in overview: Chapter 10. Disputes and treaty claims in practice, PRI, sanctions, anti-bribery: Chapter 60. Chapter 54 owns governing law, forum, enforcement of awards, immunity, stabilization and treaty structuring.
   - Bond versus loan, negative carry: Chapter 30. Market flex, fees and margins: Chapter 38. Credit paper writing: Chapter 86. Managing advisors and the personal craft of running a timetable: Chapter 87. Chapter 55 owns the financing strategy decision, the arranging process, credit approval as a process, syndication, documentation management, CP satisfaction, signing versus close, funds flow and the critical path.
   - Clause content of concessions, PPAs, EPC contracts, GSAs and O&M contracts: Chapters 17, 18, 22, 25 and 24. Chapter 56 owns negotiation method and the trade-offs on financing terms, and applies the method to those counterparties without re-teaching their clauses.
5. **Real cases.** Writers use facts only from the named fact sheets. Each "Do not state" list in those sheets binds. New facts go in the status note as verification flags.
