# Chapter 36 review, round 1: facts

Reviewer role: fact-checker (standards §12.6). Checked against facts/t-market-norms.md, t-market-norms-2.md, t-oecd-pf-2018.md, t-oecd-arrangement.md, dogger-bank.md, sh130.md, dulles-greenway.md, indiana-toll-road.md, ocean-wind.md, t-uk-cfd-rab.md, t-ratings-2.md, decisions D-007 and D-011. Primary checks: Sempra 8-K (Port Arthur, 2023-03-20) and NRF "Cost of Capital: 2024 Outlook". Web checks on new fictional names.

## Verdict: FAIL

Most real-world statements match the sheets: Port Arthur and Rio Grande terms; the NRF DSCR ladder; Comber Wind; ACWA maturities; the IFC gearing range; DBRS 1.20x; the current Arrangement Article 13(e) and CCSU limits; the 2017 Annex VII terms; Dogger Bank; Dulles Greenway; Indiana Toll Road; Ocean Wind; and the S&P OPBA reading in Solution 36.18. The chapter respects the "Do not state" lists on regional norms and rating thresholds. The defects below remain.

## Defects

1. **Opening, line 5 ("...every other limit the lenders and their export credit agencies imposed").** In context this describes Port Arthur LNG. Sempra's 8-K names commercial banks only (Mizuho as agent) and no ECA, and t-market-norms has no ECA either. The sentence suggests ECA participation that did not happen. **Fix:** "...every other limit the lenders imposed".

2. **ssec:36.4.1 line 324; Exh 36.10 row "US solar, contracted"; Solution 36.19 ("a one-year P99 near 1.0x").** The source says "1.0 times P99" without "one-year" (NRF 2024, checked; t-market-norms item 2). Only the 2025 merchant figures are stated as one-year P99. **Fix:** delete "one-year" in all three places for the 2024 contracted-solar P99 figure, e.g. "a P99 DSCR of 1.0x". Exh 36.10: "P99 about 1.0x (2024)".

3. **ssec:36.10.1 line 617 ("far below the 80%-plus seen in contracted renewables").** The sheets do not verify this as a norm. It appears only as a teaching angle in t-market-norms. The verified evidence is single deals (IFC about 62–80%; Shuweihat 80%; REIPPPP BW1–4 mostly 70–80%) and a US construction-debt figure of up to about 90% that tax equity repays. Under D-011 the phrase is an unlabeled norm. **Fix:** "...far below the 75% to 80% of cost at the DFI-led contracted wind and gas deals above (single deals, 2022–2026)", or label it indicative with market and period.

4. **Exh 36.10, row "US LNG export".** "about two-thirds (Rio Grande)" sits under "term debt about 52% of capex", so it reads as two-thirds of capex. The sheet's two-thirds is of committed term debt plus equity (t-market-norms item 20, medium confidence). **Fix:** "...about 52% of capex (Port Arthur); about two-thirds of term debt plus equity (Rio Grande)".

5. **ssec:36.10.1 line 611 and Exh 36.10 row "Mining, limited recourse".** "mining debt with no long offtake amortizes over about six to eight years after completion" has two problems. First, QB2 repayments began on June 15, 2023, before completion was reached in March 2025 (t-market-norms-2 item 37), so "after completion" is wrong for one of the two cited deals. Second, the claim generalizes from two deals. t-market-norms-2 "Do not state" forbids generic mining norms. **Fix:** "...while at two recent limited-recourse mining financings the debt amortizes over about six to eight years: Skouries in Greece (repayments 2026 to 2032) and Quebrada Blanca Phase 2 in Chile (17 equal semiannual installments from June 2023, before completion)". Exhibit row: "Amortization over about six to eight years (two deals)".

6. **sec:36.10 line 607 ("A 20-year tenor is routine for an Indian state lender").** The evidence is PFC loans to one sponsor (ReNew), plus an 11.25-year SBI refinancing. "Routine" overstates it. **Fix:** "A 20-year tenor is available from an Indian state lender (ReNew's 2023 PFC loans)...".

7. **D-011 labeling, ssec:36.5.2 line 409 and ssec:36.4.1 line 326.**
   - (a) The book's tail-length view carries a sector and period ("contracted power and infrastructure in 2023 to 2026") but no market. D-011 requires market and period, and the domain reviewer must sign off the range. **Fix:** "indicative judgments for contracted power and infrastructure financed by international commercial banks, ECAs and DFIs, 2023 to 2026"; domain reviewer sign-off still required.
   - (b) "A P50 test at 1.35x, a ten-year P90 test at 1.25x and a one-year P99 floor at 1.00x" is unlabeled and reads as a market norm. **Fix:** open with "For example, an illustrative package of...".

8. **Example 36.6 (Cheviot Edge), line 331: "a 15-year contract for difference awarded in 2025".** This conflicts with the real CfD timetable (t-uk-cfd-rab). AR6 results came in September 2024 on 15-year terms. AR7 had its application window in August 2025, with results in January and February 2026 on 20-year terms for onshore wind. No CfD was awarded in 2025. A 15-year onshore CfD awarded in 2025 cannot exist. "Indexed at 2.0% a year" also misdescribes a CPI-indexed strike. **Fix:** "a 15-year contract for difference awarded in Allocation Round 6 in 2024 ... CPI-indexed, assumed here at 2.0% a year". The alternative is a 20-year AR7 contract, but that changes the numbers.

9. **sec:36.9 line 603 and ssec:36.6.1 line 419 (2018 ECA terms).**
   - (a) "Kessara is not a high-income OECD country, so the fallback did not reach Case P" misreads the footnote. Under the fallback the maximum term stays at 14 years for everyone; only the 10-year and 5.25-year exception for ECA-heavy high-income OECD projects did not apply (t-oecd-pf-2018 item 7). The sheet does not verify whether the 7.25-year WAL survived for other countries after 2017, and its "Do not state" bars claiming an extension. **Fix:** "Kessara is not a high-income OECD country, so the 10-year and 5.25-year exception did not apply; Exportgarant's cover applied the 14-year and 7.25-year terms." This keeps the 7.25-year WAL as a term of the fictional ECA's cover, not a claim about the real Arrangement in 2018.
   - (b) Line 419, "under which 2018 commitments such as Case P's were made", should be "on which Exportgarant's 2018 cover for Case P was based".

10. **sec:36.12 line 811.**
    - "(Annex P~3.6; ...)" points to an internal production file (case-bible-annex-p.md §3.6). Readers will take it for an OECD Arrangement annex.
    - "An early draft of the Case Bible said..." also exposes production material.
    - "Six months is the Arrangement's standard rule" is true only of the 2017 text. The current main text allows up to one year (t-oecd-arrangement item 11).

    **Fix:** delete "Annex P~3.6;" and the early-draft sentence. Rewrite as: "Under the 2017 text, credits outside the project finance terms had to start repaying within six months of the starting point; the FC base profile, first repaying eight months after COD, would have failed that rule."

11. **sec:36.6.2 line 458, SH 130 lesson ("when debt is sized to a single ramp-up forecast with thin cover and no funded reserve").** sh130.md does not say whether SH 130 had a funded ramp-up reserve, and it gives no cover ratios. The sentence attributes both to the real case. **Fix:** "The transferable lesson: debt sized to a single ramp-up forecast, with back-ended repayment that assumes growth, leaves the lenders no margin for error when the first years fall short."

12. **sec:36.7.2 line 522 and Sources.** The text cites "(GIIA 2015)" but the Sources list gives "Global Infrastructure Investor Association (GIIA). n.d." and indiana-toll-road.md gives no date. **Fix:** cite "(GIIA n.d.)". The 3.4 billion figure and the swap description come from this single medium-confidence case study, so keep "approximately".

13. **Fictional parties in identifiable real processes.**
    - Ex 36.1 places a fictional 2025 financial close in a courthouse "procured by the provincial infrastructure agency" in Ontario, which identifies a real body (Infrastructure Ontario).
    - Ex 36.4 places a fictional winner in "a regulated auction contract won in 2024" in Brazil.
    - Ex 36.2 does the same with "a 2023 distribution-company auction" in Chile.

    None of these are verified. They assign invented awards to real procurements (D-007 spirit; §9 "never invent facts about real ... market data"). **Fix:** use generic wording: "procured by a provincial authority"; "a 20-year regulated auction contract"; "a 15-year dollar PPA won in a distribution-company supply auction". No year tied to a real round.

14. **Unsourced market-practice assertions.** Three claims are stated as market fact with no source:
    - Line 456: "Option (c) is the usual answer for a bank-financed toll road".
    - Line 456: "Accreting structures ... almost always with public lenders or subordinated creditors".
    - Solution 36.19: "DFIs often do [count the DSRA in the P99 test]".

    **Fix:** frame each as the book's judgment ("In this book's view, option (c)..."; "In our experience accreting structures..."; "some DFIs accept this...") or source them.
