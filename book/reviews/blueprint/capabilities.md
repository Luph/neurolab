# Blueprint review: capabilities lens

Reviewer lens: capabilities (standards Section 3). Scope: for each of the 15 capabilities, trace the chapters, worked examples, walkthroughs, exercises, model tasks, judgment drills, capstone tasks and exam questions that build it, and judge whether a reader who works the book can do it at practitioner level. Second scope: the novice path (Section 3, "assume zero knowledge"): does every chapter's assumed knowledge exist earlier?

Files read: standards.md (full); architecture.md; decisions.md; ownership-resolutions.md (index and R-008, R-011 to R-013, R-020, R-075, R-106); briefs u01 to u17 (every chapter's purpose and capability statement, concepts owned and assumed, and, for the chapters that carry the capabilities, worked examples, walkthroughs, frameworks, judgment drills and full exercise sets: Ch 1, 5, 13, 17 to 28 walkthroughs, 29 to 31, 35 to 45, 48 to 53, 55 to 65, 85, 86, matter 00 and 89 to 94); glossary-canon.md (tax entries); case-bible-annex-p.md (figure-ID map); model/case_p_report.md, model/figure-ledger-case-p.md and the sheet and row structure of model/Case_P_Model.xlsx.

## Verdict: FAIL

1 blocking, 10 major and 6 minor defects. The blocking defect is in Capability 4: the reader cannot follow the cell-by-cell build of Part VII and reconcile it to the companion workbook, because the workbook does not have the layout the Part VII briefs teach and cannot be built in their chapter order. Most other capabilities are well practiced. The major defects are missing sub-skills (drafting a full term sheet, writing a full credit paper, scoping diligence with request lists, screening fresh teasers, building an unguided model) and four places where the novice path breaks (Excel, VBA and tax concepts used before they are taught, and an Excel model task in Chapter 1).

## Defects

### 1. The Part VII cell-by-cell build cannot be followed or reconciled to the companion workbook (blocking)

- Location: bible/briefs/u09.md Section 0.1 (items 1, 2, 5), Section 0.2 (row map 0.2.3 to 0.2.14), 39.J, 40.J, 40.M (40.10, 40.13), 42.J, 42.M (42.12, 42.13), 43.M (43.10, 43.14); bible/decisions.md D-113; model/Case_P_Model.xlsx; model/case_p_report.md Section 3.
- Defect: standards Section 4.G and 7 require "cell-by-cell walkthroughs the reader can follow in a blank workbook, and a companion model". The blueprint does not deliver this:
  1. The row map in u09 Section 0.2 (the "canonical layout" every Part VII writer quotes) does not describe the delivered workbook. The workbook's Inputs sheet starts the scenario selector at F8 with a 15-scenario table (map: F4, four scenarios); the Time sheet holds only the semiannual timeline in 34 rows (the monthly timeline sits on the Construction sheet); the Funding sheet is an affine closed form (alpha plus beta times T) whose constants (rows 11 to 17) read the Debt and Operations sheets; the Debt sheet carries the waiver, deferral, LD prepayment and 2025 refinancing blocks. Section 0.1 item 2 tells each of seven writers to "re-read the row numbers from Case_P_Model.xlsx" and keep the map's "sheet names, block order and formulas" at the same time, which gives seven chapters seven partial reconciliations.
  2. The workbook cannot be built in chapter order. Chapter 40 builds Funding, but the workbook's Funding constants need the DSRA coefficient, which reads the first debt-service period's rate rows (Operations row 9, Debt rows 8 and 14) and the contract repayment profile (Debt rows 17 and 18), built in Chapters 41 and 42. A reader who builds Funding in Chapter 40 cannot reproduce P-F07 or P-F13 until Chapter 42, and nothing tells the reader how to close that loop.
  3. The chapters teach macros that the companion does not contain. Chapters 40, 42 and 43 teach Converge, LockDebt, RunScenarios and RunMonteCarlo and set exercises on them (40.10, 40.13, 42.12, 43.10, 43.14), but D-113 and case_p_report.md Section 3 state the workbook "contains no circular reference and needs no iterative calculation or macro". The workbook also carries the sculpted repayment profile as an input (re-sizing is done only in Python), so the reader has no Excel reference for re-sizing debt live or for the Monte Carlo loop.
  4. Section 0.2.4 and 39.J step 3 still teach the single mixed timeline with stub columns and Equation (39.2) in its stub form, while Section 0.1 item 5 says the companion has no stubs and a band-overlap rule "replaces Equation (39.2)". Exercises 39.6 and 39.10 drill the replaced design.
- Required fix:
  1. Commission the Case P modeler to produce a build-along workbook series `model/build/Case_P_ch39.xlsx` to `Case_P_ch43.xlsx` (plus `.xlsm` versions from Chapter 40 on), each the state of the model at the end of that chapter, built in chapter order and reconciling at every stage to the Python mirror (add `verify_build.py`). Where a later sheet is needed earlier (the DSRA coefficient in Chapter 40), the Chapter 40 file uses a documented placeholder input row (the contract repayment profile pasted as an input, labelled "replaced by the live link in Chapter 42"), and Chapter 42 replaces it and shows that the totals do not move.
  2. Include the four macros (Converge, LockDebt, RunScenarios, RunMonteCarlo) in the `.xlsm` files, with a sizing-mode switch that re-sculpts in Excel, and add their converged outputs to the ledger (pass count and residual already requested as P-F43; Monte Carlo as P-F42).
  3. Regenerate u09 Section 0.2 mechanically from those files (sheet, row, label, first-column formula) and mark it binding. Delete the stale map. Writers quote only regenerated addresses.
  4. In 39.D, 39.F, 39.J and 39.M, teach the two-timeline design with the band-overlap rule as the book's model and keep the mixed timeline with stubs as a clearly labelled alternative. Rewrite Exercises 39.6 and 39.10 so that the solution states both designs' answers.
  5. Update `matter/00` (fm:model-builds) to list the build-along files and say which file each Tier 3 task starts from.
