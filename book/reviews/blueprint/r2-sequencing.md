### 1. Major: the Chapter 8 brief prints a P-F64 bridge that contradicts ledger v1.4

- Location: `bible/briefs/u02.md` line 1170 (Ch 8 Case P "Figures to display"), against `model/figure-ledger-case-p.md` lines 1200 to 1211 (P-F64). The same issue appears in lesser form at u10:125 (ssec:46.2.4).
- Defect:
  - The brief describes the bridge in the wrong direction. It runs "cumulative steps from the FC base 13.3% through capex +1.8 points, financing terms +0.1, fees and premiums +1.6, tax +0.1, FX and indexation +0.8, schedule +0.1, other bid-model differences (1.7) to the 16.0% bid-model IRR".
  - Ledger v1.4 (D-115, sequential) runs the other way, from the 16.00% bid model to the FC base 13.27%. Its steps are:

    - debt terms +0.44
    - PRI and WHT gross-up −0.86
    - soft mini-perm −0.14
    - capex −1.80
    - VAT facility interest −0.05
    - tax −0.06
    - FX −0.79
    - IRR dating −0.05
  - The ledger states the total as −2.73 pp with "no residual".
  - Only capex and FX match the brief. The brief's "financing terms", "fees and premiums" and "schedule" amounts, and its 1.7-point residual, do not exist in the ledger.
  - u10:125 names step categories (financing terms, fees and premiums, schedule) that are also not the ledger's. It at least tells the writer to print the steps "as the ledger gives it".
- Fix:
  - In u02:1170, replace the parenthesis after "P-F64" with: "(sequential bid-to-close equity IRR bridge, from the 16.0% Kilnworth bid model to the FC base 13.3%; print the step labels and values only as `model/figure-ledger-case-p.md` gives them; no residual line)".
