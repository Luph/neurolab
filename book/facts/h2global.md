# H2Global: the Hintco double-auction mechanism and the first tender results (2024) and follow-on rounds

As of: 2026-10-03 (latest event covered: Hintco announcement that AM Green won the Asian regional lot of the second H2Global tender, 30 September 2026)

Cross-references: facts/t-hydrogen-support.md (UK HAR1, the EU Hydrogen Bank auctions and US 45V; read it for comparison with other support schemes, as this sheet does not repeat them); facts/neom-hydrogen.md (a merchant-export green ammonia project); facts/saf-mandates.md (why the e-SAF lot failed: regulatory GHG accounting); facts/t-uk-cfd-rab.md (the CfD logic H2Global adapts).

## Summary

H2Global is a German-originated mechanism for importing renewable hydrogen derivatives (RFNBO ammonia, methanol, e-fuels). A government-funded intermediary, HINT.CO GmbH (Hintco), Leipzig, a subsidiary of the non-profit H2Global Foundation, buys and sells the product through two separate auctions:

- on the supply side, it signs long-term Hydrogen Purchase Agreements (HPAs) with the lowest-cost producers worldwide;
- on the demand side, it resells the product in short-term Hydrogen Sales Agreements (HSAs) to the European buyers willing to pay the most;
- the gap between purchase and sale prices is covered by public grants, mainly from the German federal economy ministry.

The producer therefore gets a creditworthy, long-term, fixed-price offtaker, which is the bankability piece. The state pays only the "cost of difference", and the sales auctions produce price discovery.

The EUR 900 million pilot auction, launched at the end of 2022, had three lots:
- Lot 1, ammonia: Fertiglobe (the ADNOC and OCI partnership) won with a delivered contract price of EUR 1,000 per tonne (net ex-works EUR 811 per tonne), announced in July 2024. Supply comes from the 100 MW Egypt Green Hydrogen project at Ain Sokhna, starting with up to 19,500 tonnes in 2027 and reaching up to 397,500 tonnes by 2033, for a maximum contract value of EUR 397 million.
- Lot 3, e-SAF: no award, because of GHG-accounting rules for Fischer-Tropsch co-products and a contract size too small for a new plant.
- Lot 2, methanol: relaunched in 2025 with EUR 437.5 million.

A second tender of at least EUR 2.5 billion (EU state-aid approval for up to EUR 3 billion) launched on 19 February 2025. It has four regional lots of EUR 580 million each and a global lot co-funded by Germany and the Netherlands. The first regional winner, AM Green (India) for the Asian lot, was announced on 30 September 2026: a 10-year contract worth at least EUR 585 million, with deliveries from 2029.

## Verified facts

1. Structure: the H2Global Foundation is a non-profit organisation, and Hintco is its wholly owned subsidiary and trading intermediary. Hintco signs long-term HPAs with producers, offering "price, market, counterparty, and legal security". It resells the product through subsequent sales auctions under short-term HSAs to the highest bidders. The expected gap between purchase and sale prices ("cost of difference") is covered by grants from public or philanthropic funders. Hintco compares the model to a contract for difference. [confidence: high] [source: 1]
2. The pilot auction was EUR 900 million, financed by the German Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Climate Action (BMWK), and launched at the end of 2022. It had three lots: renewable ammonia (Lot 1), renewable methanol (Lot 2) and e-SAF (Lot 3). [confidence: high] [source: 2, 3]
3. Lot 1 procedure: a two-stage negotiated procedure, with a qualification phase followed by negotiation and bidding, capped at five final bidders.
   - Hundreds of parties from 65 countries downloaded the documents, and more than 1,400 questions were asked.
   - 22 companies or consortia requested to participate, and five, from Asia, North Africa, the Middle East and South America, were invited to bid.
   - The bid deadline was extended four times, finally to 19 May (2024).
   - The HPA was redrafted during negotiations to reflect the RED II delegated acts on RFNBOs and the EU CBAM.

   Hintco noted that a binding bid de facto requires the bidder to be at FID: advanced FEED, basic lender commitment and indicative supplier prices. [confidence: high] [source: 3]
4. Aggregate Lot 1 bid data:
   - average electrolysis capacity of 145 MW and average new renewables of 295 MW;
   - offered volumes of 210,000 to 475,000 tonnes;
   - all bids used alkaline electrolysers;
   - average net product price of EUR 1,048 per tonne across bids including initial ones;
   - the net product price averaged 81% of the contract price, with transport at 9%, logistics at 7% and import duties at 3%;
   - one bid exceeded the price cap. [confidence: high] [source: 3]
5. Winner: Fertiglobe PLC, a strategic partnership between ADNOC and OCI, listed on the Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange. It will source renewable hydrogen from Egypt Green Hydrogen, a consortium of Fertiglobe, Scatec ASA, Orascom Construction, the Sovereign Fund of Egypt and the Egyptian Electricity Transmission Company, in the Suez Canal Economic Zone. Hintco announced the result in a press release dated "07.11.2024", and S&P Global reported it on 11 July 2024. [confidence: high for the result; medium-high for the 11 July 2024 date] [source: 2, 3, 4]
6. Price: a contract price of EUR 1,000.00 per tonne delivered to Europe (including transport, logistics and customs, excluding VAT) and a net (ex-works) price of EUR 811.30 per tonne, volume-weighted over the firm quantities. The maximum permissible net product price (bid cap) was EUR 1,282 per tonne (EUR 1.28 per kg), and Fertiglobe's net price was 37% below it. [confidence: high] [source: 3]
7. Volumes and value:
   - contractual offtake commitment: 19,500 tonnes in 2027, then 40,000 tonnes a year from 2028, a total of 259,500 tonnes to 2033;
   - maximum supply including Hintco's options (17,500 to 33,000 tonnes a year): up to 73,000 tonnes a year from 2028, a total of 397,500 tonnes;
   - maximum contract value: EUR 397 million;
   - the guaranteed minimum offtake is based on Hintco's available purchase budget of EUR 40 million a year. [confidence: high] [source: 2, 3]
8. Project:
   - a pressurised alkaline electrolyser of 100 MW producing up to 13,000 tonnes a year of renewable hydrogen;
   - an upgraded existing Haber-Bosch plant producing up to 74,000 tonnes a year of ammonia, operating by 2027 and at full capacity in early 2028;
   - power from a new 203 MW onshore wind farm at Ras Ghareb and a new 70 MWp solar plant at Benban, transmitted over the Egyptian national grid;
   - monthly matching of renewable generation to consumption until 31 December 2029, then hourly matching, per EU rules;
   - delivery to Rotterdam, with Hintco selling standardised quantities in annual HSA auctions.

   Emissions intensity is approximately 75.5% below grey ammonia (0.432 tCO2e per tonne of NH3, about 23 gCO2e/MJ). The electrolyser manufacturer had not been chosen at award. [confidence: high] [source: 3]
9. Subsidy mechanics: the subsidy depends on later sales auctions. BMWK pays Hintco only the difference between purchase price and sale price. The first sales auction was expected in 2025/26. [confidence: high (as of the 2024 brief)] [source: 3]
10. Lot 3 (e-SAF) ended without an award:
    - over 300 parties from 43 countries downloaded the documents, and three qualified;
    - only one submitted an indicative bid, and it declined to make a final offer;
    - the reasons given were uncertainty in the regulatory GHG accounting for carbon used in e-SAF, and a contract value and duration too small for a new plant;
    - the remaining bidder needed to allocate renewable hydrogen solely to e-SAF, but the Commission's reading of RED II delegated act 28(5) requires proportional allocation across all Fischer-Tropsch products.

    The Lot 3 funds were moved to Lot 2 (methanol). [confidence: high] [source: 2, 3]
11. Second round:
    - 18 December 2024: Hintco announced EU approval of a second auction round worth up to EUR 3 billion;
    - 13 January 2025: Germany committed EUR 588 million to bilateral H2Global tenders with Australia and Canada;
    - 19 February 2025: Hintco launched the second tender of EUR 2.5 billion, with four regional lots (Asia; Africa; North America; South America and Oceania) of at least EUR 484 million each, and a global lot of at least EUR 567 million co-funded by Germany and the Netherlands, the first multi-government H2Global auction. The lots are product-open RFNBO (ammonia, methanol, hydrogen). [confidence: high] [source: 5, 6]
12. On 22 September 2025 Hintco raised each regional lot from EUR 484 million to EUR 580 million (EUR 58 million a year over ten years). It moved the regional funding period to 2029-2038, subject to budget approval in December 2025. It set Germany's global-lot contribution at EUR 300 million (funding from 2032) and the Netherlands' funding period at 2028-2036. It relaunched the methanol pilot lot with EUR 437.5 million. The regional application phase had closed on 12 September 2025. [confidence: high] [source: 7]
13. On 30 September 2026 Hintco named AM Green Ammonia (India) Private Limited winner of the Asian regional lot, for RFNBO ammonia. The award is a 10-year contract with a minimum value of EUR 585 million and deliveries from 2029. Supply will come from the converted Phase 2 natural gas ammonia and urea complex at Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh, using alkaline electrolysis and round-the-clock renewable power backed by pumped-hydro storage. Hintco said results for the African, North American and South American/Oceanian lots would be published before the end of 2026. [confidence: high] [source: 8]

## Financing and structure details

- Offtaker: Hintco, a Leipzig GmbH, buying under HPAs. Its creditworthiness rests on a grant agreement with the German federal government; the pilot was financed by BMWK.
- Contract shape (Fertiglobe): fixed delivered price of EUR 1,000 per tonne; a firm minimum volume of 40,000 tonnes a year (2028-2033) sized to a EUR 40 million annual budget; Hintco options for more; a term to 2033, about 6.5 years including the 2027 ramp-up. Later rounds use 10-year funding periods.
- Resale: annual HSA auctions to European buyers at market-discovered prices; the government funds the difference.
- Producer-side financing: Egypt Green Hydrogen's lenders and FID were not verified (see Do not state).

## What went wrong or right, and why

- Right: Hintco's own brief presents the pilot as producing the first public price signal for imported RFNBO ammonia (EUR 1,000 per tonne delivered, EUR 811 per tonne ex-works), 37% below the cap (source 3).
- Limits: the e-SAF lot failed for two stated reasons (source 3). Rules on allocating GHG savings across Fischer-Tropsch co-products undermined product economics, and the contract value and term were too small relative to plant capex and development time. One ammonia bidder also withdrew because the procurement volume did not justify the investment. Later rounds responded with larger lots, ten-year funding periods and product-open lots (sources 5, 7).

## Teaching angles by chapter

- Ch 83 (hydrogen, CCS and sustainable fuels): use H2Global as the clearest example of a "market-risk holder" design. The producer takes construction and operating risk against a fixed-price, long-term, state-backed offtake. The intermediary carries resale price risk, which the government grant absorbs as a cost of difference. European buyers take only short-term exposure. Contrast it with the EU Hydrogen Bank's fixed premium and the UK HAR1 CfD (facts/t-hydrogen-support.md). Have readers rebuild the delivered price from the 81/9/7/3 cost split, and compute the subsidy at different resale prices. For example, at a resale price of EUR 600 per tonne, the gap is EUR 400 per tonne; at 40,000 tonnes a year this is EUR 16 million a year of grant, against EUR 40 million a year of purchase cost (40,000 tonnes at EUR 1,000), which matches Hintco's stated EUR 40 million purchase budget.

## Do not state

- Do not state that Egypt Green Hydrogen has reached FID or financial close, or name its lenders. Fertiglobe targeted FID for H1 2025, and later reports conflict; EIB and EU grant involvement is reported only in trade press and was not verified.
- Do not state the outcome of any HSA sales auction (resale prices), or that one has occurred. Neither was verified.
- Do not state the AM Green contract price or volumes. They were not disclosed in the source.
- Do not state the outcome of the methanol lot, the global lot or the Australia and Canada bilateral tenders. None was verified as of the as-of date.
- Do not state Hintco's HPA legal terms (take-or-pay mechanics, penalty levels, currency) beyond what is listed above.
- Do not describe the German ministry by a single name across periods. The pilot was funded by BMWK, and the ministry was renamed in 2025; check the current name before writing.
