# Indicative O&M agreement and LTSA terms, 2022–2026: fee structures, availability guarantees, liability limits and terms (from public filings)

As of: 2026-10-03 (latest documents used: Kenon Holdings 6-K with OPC Energy interim statements, 12 August 2026; YPF Luz F-1/A, 23 July 2026; Ellomay 20-F for 2025, filed 1 May 2026)

Cross-references: OEM fleet defects and owners' remedies under LTSAs are in facts/oem-fleet-defects.md. Construction-contract norms are in facts/t-construction-norms.md. Lender-side operating-period norms (DSCR, reserves) are in facts/t-market-norms.md and facts/t-market-norms-2.md.

## Summary

Public filings rarely give a complete O&M or LTSA term sheet, but together they show a consistent architecture.

- **Operator O&M agreements** for thermal plants usually pay a modest fixed annual fee plus pass-through of payroll and operating costs, with a capped performance adjustment tied to safety, environment, budget and availability.
  - The Guernsey O&M agreement (Ohio, approximately 1,875 MW CCGT, 2019) paid a USD 305,000 annual fee plus reimbursement, with an annual performance adjustment around a USD 183,000 base.
  - Cheniere's Sabine Pass affiliates pay cost reimbursement plus a fixed, inflation-indexed fee per LNG train.
- **OEM LTSAs (CSAs)** for gas turbines run for long terms tied to operating hours or major inspections. They pay a fixed monthly fee plus a variable fee per fired hour, and the OEM gives availability or reliability guarantees backed by LDs and offset by owner-paid bonuses.
  - Guernsey's 25-year GE CSA: USD 25,000 a month plus USD 500 per gas-turbine fired hour, both escalating 2.5% a year, plus about USD 20 million for initial spares.
  - OPC's Hadera Expansion CSA with GE Vernova (Israel, 2026): an estimated USD 141 million over up to 30 years, with a reliability guarantee, a capped bonus and a GE Vernova parent guarantee.
- **Solar O&M** is a fixed subscription. Ellomay pays EUR 15,000 to 28,000 per MWp a year for Spanish plants and about EUR 3,000 per MWp for Texas plants.
- **Liability caps and guarantee percentages** are almost never disclosed. Filings say only that guarantees are "subject to certain agreed limitations on liability".

## Verified facts

### A. Thermal operator O&M agreements

1. Guernsey Power Station (Ohio, USA; approximately 1,875 MW combined cycle; commercial operations 1 May 2023). The O&M agreement (OMA) with Ethos Energy Power Plant Services, LLC is dated 13 May 2019 and has two phases:
   - **Mobilization Phase** (before COD): USD 10,000 a month plus reimbursement of payroll and other operating costs.
   - **Operational Phase**: USD 305,000 annual fee, paid monthly, plus reimbursement of payroll and other operating costs.
   An annual performance adjustment can run either way, payable by the owner to the operator or by the operator to the owner. It uses an escalated annual base amount of USD 183,000 and considers operator performance on safety, environmental and budget compliance and facility availability.

   The OMA expires 5 years after the start of the Operational Phase, extendable by mutual agreement. The owner may terminate without cause during the Operational Phase on 60 days' notice. Fees incurred in 2024 were USD 424,000. [confidence: high] [source: 1]
2. Sabine Pass LNG (Cheniere Energy Partners), 2025 10-K. The project companies have no employees and use services agreements with Cheniere affiliates. Payment "primarily consist[s] of cost reimbursement plus a compensating fee based on a fixed amount (indexed for inflation) per Train in service". Before a train reaches substantial completion, the fee is "a percentage of the capital expenditures of the Train under construction". Affiliate O&M expense under the services agreements was USD 177 million in 2025, against USD 172 million in 2024. [confidence: high] [source: 2]
3. Dorad Power Plant (Israel; Ellomay holds an indirect stake). The O&M agreement runs 24 years and 11 months from the permanent licence, but no longer than the site lease. Payment is "a fixed and variable (depending on production during the period) monthly payment", and the agreement covers spare parts and repairs. The O&M contractor (Edeltech O&M) is linked to shareholders of Dorad, so it is a related-party O&M arrangement. [confidence: high] [source: 3]

### B. Gas turbine LTSAs and CSAs

4. Guernsey Contractual Service Agreement (CSA) with General Electric International, Inc., dated 28 June 2019:
   - **Term:** 25 years initial.
   - **Scope:** maintenance, repair of collateral damage, initial spare parts, monitoring systems, unscheduled outage obligations, non-hazardous clean-up and permits for the turbines and generators.
   - **Initial spares:** USD 20 million plus escalation, in four annual payments. The first payment, USD 5.944 million, was made on 31 October 2024.
   - **Fees:** a monthly fixed fee of USD 25,000 and a variable fee of USD 500 per gas turbine fired hour. Both escalate 2.5% a year. [confidence: high] [source: 1]
5. OPC Energy, Hadera Expansion (Israel, approximately 850 MW CCGT under construction). In February 2026 Hadera Expansion signed a CSA with GE Vernova alongside the main equipment supply agreement:
   - **Cost:** estimated at approximately USD 141 million in total, linked to various indices.
   - **Term:** expires 30 years after signing or on completion of the required maintenance (measured by parameters in the agreement), whichever comes first. An early-termination mechanism is included.
   - **Guarantees and bonus:** a reliability guarantee and other performance undertakings, including on emissions, with compensation if missed. The owner pays bonuses for overperformance "up to an aggregate cap for each assessment period".
   - **Security:** GE Vernova (Switzerland) GmbH gives a corporate guarantee. The owner provides a company guarantee or, alternatively, a dedicated maintenance reserve account. [confidence: high] [source: 4]
6. YPF Luz (Argentina) discloses LTSAs with GE Vernova in which "GE Vernova has committed to guarantee a minimum availability of the plant". Disclosed terms:
   - San Miguel de Tucumán: to September 2028.
   - El Bracho: the later of the first major inspection of the gas and steam turbines and October 2030, the 10th anniversary of COD.
   - Loma Campana I (LMS100): the later of December 2037, 100,000 operating hours, or completion of the agreement overhaul. Major maintenance and performance guarantees, spare parts, repairs and field services are included.
   - Loma Campana II (LMS100): the later of December 2038, 100,000 operating hours, or completion of the agreement overhaul.
   - Tucumán: separate Sulzer LTSAs for overhauls of major rotating equipment, expiring December 2028.

   Loma Campana I ran "significantly below the availability guarantee" after a forced outage from May 2023 to August 2024. YPF Luz began ICC arbitration against GE Vernova affiliates in August 2024. [confidence: high] [source: 5]
7. Venture Global (US LNG), 2024 10-K:
   - Venture Global can require Baker Hughes to enter LTSAs "on specified terms" for the liquefaction, power and booster compression equipment it supplies. It exercised this for Calcasieu Pass (signed December 2022) and Plaquemines (signed December 2024).
   - Baker Hughes guarantees "a minimum annual operating availability", with LDs payable if availability falls short. Venture Global pays a bonus where availability exceeds a set level.
   - Both sides' obligations are "subject to certain agreed limitations on liability". [confidence: high] [source: 6]
8. GE's 2018 Form 10-K shows the provider's side of a fixed-price LTSA. In Q4 2018 GE took USD 0.4 billion of pre-tax charges for "assumption updates for unfavorable pricing, lower utilization, and cost updates on our long-term service agreements". [confidence: high] [source: 7]

### C. Renewable O&M and service agreements

9. Ellomay solar plants (2025 20-F). Each plant has an O&M agreement with a local contractor.
   - **Scope:** "Subscription Services" (preventive and corrective maintenance, cleaning, vegetation, surveillance, remote supervision) and separately priced "Non-Subscription Services".
   - **Spanish plants** (other than Talasol and the Ellomay Solar plants): annual subscription fees of EUR 15,000 to 28,000 per MWp, CPI-linked, paid quarterly.
   - **Talasol** (300 MW): approximately EUR 2.4 million a year, paid monthly.
   - **Texas solar plants:** approximately EUR 3,000 per MWp a year.
   - **Fee and lender terms:** the fee is fixed, with no increase for unforeseen circumstances, and excludes insurance. Lender approval is generally required to replace an O&M contractor, and a lender-appointed technical adviser monitors performance in several financings. [confidence: high] [source: 3]
10. Kibby Wind (132 MW, Franklin County, Maine; REV Renewables S-1/A, December 2022). The Service and Maintenance Agreement with Vestas-American Wind Technology covers all scheduled and unscheduled maintenance, diagnostics, repair and replacement. It is paid by fixed quarterly fee and expires 22 October 2029. [confidence: high] [source: 8]

## Market norms: indicative structures (what the sources support)

| Element | Indicative structure (market, period) | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Operator O&M fee (thermal) | Fixed annual fee plus cost pass-through (payroll, opex); capped two-way performance adjustment on safety, environment, budget and availability (US CCGT, 2019–2024) | Guernsey [1]; Cheniere per-train indexed fee [2] |
| Operator O&M term | 5 years renewable, owner termination for convenience on short notice (US merchant CCGT); up to about 25 years for related-party O&M (Israel) | [1], [3] |
| OEM LTSA/CSA fee | Fixed monthly fee plus variable fee per fired hour (or per factored hour), escalating at a fixed rate (2.5% a year at Guernsey); upfront payment for initial spares | [1], [4] |
| LTSA term | 25–30 years or tied to operating hours or major inspections (for example, 100,000 hours) (US, Israel, Argentina, 2017–2026) | [1], [4], [5] |
| Availability/reliability guarantee | OEM guarantees minimum availability, paying LDs/compensation; owner pays capped bonus for overperformance (US LNG 2022–2024; Israel 2026; Argentina) | [4], [5], [6] |
| Credit support | OEM parent guarantee; owner company guarantee or maintenance reserve account (Israel 2026) | [4] |
| Solar O&M | Fixed subscription, EUR 15,000–28,000 per MWp a year (Spain) and about EUR 3,000 per MWp (Texas), 2025 | [3] |
| Lender controls | Approval to replace O&M contractor; technical adviser monitoring; O&M agreement pledged as security | [3] |

Drivers that move these terms:
- the OEM's market power, which is high in gas turbines in 2024–2026 given backlogs (see t-construction-norms.md);
- technology maturity and fleet history (oem-fleet-defects.md);
- the owner's dispatch profile, because fired-hour fees shift cost to high-utilisation plants;
- labour markets and inflation indexation;
- whether the operator is a related party;
- lenders' requirements for replaceability and reserve accounts.

## Financing and structure details

- Hadera Expansion's senior facility from Bank Leumi is approximately USD 1.7 billion, with senior debt expected at approximately 80% of construction cost. The owner's LTSA obligations are secured by a company guarantee or a dedicated maintenance reserve account, which mirrors the major maintenance reserve lenders often require. [4]
- At Guernsey, the GE equipment supply agreement was assigned to the EPC contractor (Gemma Power Systems), while the GE CSA stayed directly with the owner. Construction wrap and long-term service obligations therefore sit with different counterparties. [1]

## What went wrong or right, and why

- **LTSAs as fixed-price bets for the OEM.** GE's 2018 LTSA charge (USD 0.4 billion) came from unfavourable pricing, lower utilisation and cost updates. Fixed-fee LTSAs transfer long-dated cost risk to the OEM, which may later seek to recover it through pricing elsewhere. [7]
- **Availability guarantees are compensation, not insurance.** Loma Campana I shows that an LTSA availability guarantee did not prevent a 15-month outage. It gave the owner a claim, now in arbitration, while the offtaker gained a PPA termination option. [5]
- **Replaceability is a lender's core concern.** Ellomay's disclosure states that lender approval is generally needed to replace an O&M contractor and that a replacement might not offer the same terms and warranties. [3]

## Teaching angles by chapter

- **Ch 24 (Operations and maintenance contracts):**
  - Build Case P's O&M and LTSA from the disclosed shapes: a Guernsey-style operator fee with a two-way performance adjustment, and a GE-style CSA with fixed plus per-fired-hour fees and 2.5% escalation.
  - Model how the per-hour fee changes the LTSA's cost for a peaker against a baseload plant.
  - Teach availability guarantees and the bonus/LD symmetry (Venture Global, Hadera Expansion), and the security package on both sides (OEM parent guarantee against the owner's maintenance reserve account).
  - Use Loma Campana I to show the gap between LTSA remedies and PPA exposure.

## Do not state

- Any specific guaranteed availability percentage (for example "97%") or LD rate for the LTSAs above. None was disclosed.
- Liability caps for O&M or LTSA contracts, such as "100% of annual fee" or "one to two times annual fees", even though such rules of thumb are common in market commentary. No primary source in this research gave a figure.
- Wind turbine service agreement price ranges (EUR or USD per MW per year) or typical availability guarantee levels for full-service wind contracts. Not verified.
- Lender-presentation or independent engineer statements about "typical" O&M terms. None were retrieved in this research.
- That the Hadera Expansion CSA total of approximately USD 141 million is the full maintenance cost of the plant. It is the estimated CSA consideration only.
