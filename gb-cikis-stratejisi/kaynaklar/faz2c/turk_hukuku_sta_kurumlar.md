## Q1. Speed of legal levers

### 1a. Constitution (Law 2709), Art 73(4) and Art 167(2) — [VERIFIED]
Source: https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=2709&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5 (consolidated text, iframe https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/anasayfa/MevzuatFihristDetayIframe?MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatNo=2709&MevzuatTertip=5)

- Art 73(3): "Vergi, resim, harç ve benzeri mali yükümlülükler kanunla konulur, değiştirilir veya kaldırılır."
- Art 73(4): "Vergi, resim, harç ve benzeri mali yükümlülüklerin muaflık, istisnalar ve indirimleriyle oranlarına ilişkin hükümlerinde kanunun belirttiği yukarı ve aşağı sınırlar içinde değişiklik yapmak yetkisi Cumhurbaşkanına verilebilir." Footnote [25]: 6771 sayılı Kanun (21/1/2017) Art 16 replaced "Bakanlar Kuruluna" with "Cumhurbaşkanına".
- Art 167(1): "Devlet, para, kredi, sermaye, mal ve hizmet piyasalarının sağlıklı ve düzenli işlemelerini sağlayıcı ve geliştirici tedbirleri alır; piyasalarda fiili veya anlaşma sonucu doğacak tekelleşme ve kartelleşmeyi önler."
- Art 167(2): "Dış ticaretin ülke ekonomisinin yararına olmak üzere düzenlenmesi amacıyla ithalat, ihracat ve diğer dış ticaret işlemleri üzerine vergi ve benzeri yükümlülükler dışında ek mali yükümlülükler koymaya ve bunları kaldırmaya kanunla Cumhurbaşkanına yetki verilebilir." Footnote [111]: same 6771/16 substitution.
- Reading: rates can be moved by Presidential decision only within the band the statute sets (Art 73(4)); for foreign trade, Art 167(2) lets a law authorise the President to impose and remove "additional financial obligations" other than taxes. Law 2976 is that authorising law (see 1b).

### 1b. Customs tariff powers

#### Law 474 — correct title "Gümrük Giriş Tarife Cetveli Hakkında Kanun" (not "Gümrük Tarife Kanunu") — [VERIFIED]
Source: https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=474&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5 . Adopted 14/5/1964, RG 25/5/1964 No. 11711.
- Art 1(2): The President has power to "genişletmeye, açmaya, değiştirmeye" the positions, sub-positions and notes of the schedule, which is based on the Harmonized System.
- Art 2(1) (as amended by 7061/28, 28/11/2017; "Bakanlar Kurulu" changed to "Cumhurbaşkanı" by KHK 700/49): "Cumhurbaşkanı, bu Kanunun eki Gümrük Giriş Tarife Cetvelinde yer alan eşyaların gümrük vergisi had ve nispetlerini 50'ye kadar yükseltebilir, sıfıra kadar indirebilir veya bu Cetveldeki had ve nispetleri %50'sine kadar artırabilir."
- Art 2(2): "Türkiye'nin taraf olduğu uluslararası anlaşma hükümleri saklıdır." International agreements, including WTO bindings, CU Decision 1/95 and FTAs, therefore prevail over these powers.
- Art 5: a preparatory inter-ministerial commission meets at intervals of no less than 15 days and no more than two months.
- Interpretation [PARTIAL]: the presidential ceiling is relative to the statutory schedule annexed to Law 474. The President may raise a rate to 50 (ad valorem) or raise a statutory rate by up to 50% of itself, and may cut any rate to zero. Decision 3351 Art 2(3), below, caps "MFN + İGV" at "474 sayılı Kanunda gösterilen hadlerin %50 artırılmış düzeyi".

#### Customs Law 4458 — [VERIFIED]
Source: https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=4458&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5 . Adopted 27/10/1999, RG 4/11/1999 No. 23866.
- Art 15(1): "Gümrük vergileri, gümrük yükümlülüğünün başladığı tarihte yürürlükte olan gümrük tarifesine göre hesaplanır."
- Art 15(3): the "Gümrük Tarifesi" comprises: (a) "Cumhurbaşkanınca kabul edilen Türk Gümrük Tarife Cetvelini" (KHK 700/126 substitution); (c) duty rates; (d) preferential tariff measures under agreements with countries or groups; (e) unilateral preferences (e.g. GSP); (f) conditional exemptions and reductions.
- Art 15(5)(b): tariff ceilings end "Cumhurbaşkanı Kararı ile".
- Art 16(1): end-use preferential treatment is subject to conditions the President sets.
- Art 17: non-preferential origin, which also governs measures set by Presidential decision.
- Art 22: preferential origin rules are set (a) by the agreements themselves and (b) for unilateral preferences, by Presidential decision.
- Art 15(7): the tariff schedule is prepared by the "Müsteşarlık" (now the Ministry of Trade) and published in the RG.

#### Law 2976 Dış Ticaretin Düzenlenmesi Hakkında Kanun — the law that implements Constitution Art 167 — [VERIFIED]
Source: https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=2976&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5 . Adopted 2/2/1984, RG 15/2/1984 No. 18313.
- Art 1: the law regulates "ithalat, ihracat ve diğer dış ticaret işlemleri üzerine vergi ve benzeri yükümlülükler dışında ek mali yükümlülükler konulması ve kaldırılması" and the use of the resulting funds.
- Art 2: "Cumhurbaşkanı bu Kanun kapsamındaki konularda düzenlemeler yapmaya yetkilidir."
- Art 3: "İthalat, ihracat veya dış ticaret işlemleri üzerine konulan ek mali yükümlülüklerin nevi, miktarı, tahsili, takibi, iadesi, gerektiğinde bütçeye irat kaydedilmesi, bir fonda toplanması ve fonun kullanım esasları Cumhurbaşkanı kararında gösterilir."
- Art 4: a penalty of twice the unpaid amount.
- The law sets no numerical ceiling.

#### İlave Gümrük Vergisi (İGV) — [VERIFIED]
Legal instrument: "İthalatta İlave Gümrük Vergisi Uygulanmasına İlişkin Karar", Cumhurbaşkanı Kararı No. 3351, RG 31/12/2020 No. 31351 (3. Mükerrer), in force 1/1/2021.
- Cover page (rendered from RG PDF https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2020/12/20201231M3-2.pdf): "Ekli 'İthalatta İlave Gümrük Vergisi Uygulanmasına İlişkin Karar'ın yürürlüğe konulmasına, 1567 sayılı Kanunun 1 inci, 474 sayılı Kanunun 2 nci, 3283 sayılı Kanunun 2 nci, 4458 sayılı Kanunun 16 ncı, 22 nci ve 55 inci maddeleri ile 2976 sayılı Kanun hükümleri gereğince karar verilmiştir."
- Consolidated text: https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/anasayfa/MevzuatFihristDetayIframe?MevzuatTur=20&MevzuatNo=3351&MevzuatTertip=5
  - Art 1: İGV applies to goods in Lists II and III of the İthalat Rejimi Kararı.
  - Art 2(2): non-EU, non-Turkish-origin goods imported with an A.TR pay the "Diğer Ülkeler" İGV rate. Goods originating in FTA partners within a diagonal-cumulation system are exempt when preferential origin is proven.
  - Art 2(3), as amended by CK-8044 from 1/1/2024: "Her halükarda hesaplanan toplam vergi oranı (...gümrük vergisi ile bu Karar kapsamında alınacak ilave gümrük vergisi toplamı) 14/5/1964 tarihli ve 474 sayılı Gümrük Giriş Tarife Cetveli Hakkında Kanunda gösterilen hadlerin % 50 artırılmış düzeyini aşamaz."
  - Art 2(4): collected separately and recorded as general budget revenue.
  - Art 3: the Ministry of Trade may issue tebliğs.
  - Art 4(2): no İGV when goods leave for the EU under an A.TR after duty is paid.
  - Amended 14+ times (2021–2025). A further amendment, Karar 11508 (RG 11/7/2026 No. 33307), is reported [PARTIAL: alomaliye/IKV summaries].
- İthalat Rejimi Kararı, Karar 3350 (same RG 31/12/2020) — [VERIFIED]:
  - Standing base import regime (not re-issued annually), in force since 1/1/2021. https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/anasayfa/MevzuatFihristDetayIframe?MevzuatTur=20&MevzuatNo=3350&MevzuatTertip=5
  - Cover page legal basis is identical to the İGV decision: 1567/1, 474/2, 3283/2, 4458/16, 22, 55, and 2976. 1567 = Türk Parasının Kıymetini Koruma Hakkında Kanun [PARTIAL: title from memory; mevzuat 302]. 3283 = Bazı Kanunlarla Tanınmış Olan Gümrük Muafiyetlerinin Kaldırılması Hakkında Kanun, whose Art 2 empowers the President to grant duty exemptions [VERIFIED].
  - Art 3: the Ministry of Trade may issue tebliğs and permits, "ithalatın her aşamasında gerekli görülecek değişiklikleri yapmaya ve önlemleri almaya".
  - Art 4(2): "Türkiye ile ticari ilişkilerinde ... ayırıcı işlemler uygulayan ülkeler ... hakkında taraf olunan uluslararası anlaşmalar çerçevesinde uygun görülecek gerekli önlemler alınır."
  - Art 4(3) lists the trade-policy instruments: AD/CVD, safeguards, quota/TRQ, surveillance, textiles, and the Türkiye'nin ticari haklarının korunması mevzuatı.
  - Toplu Konut Fonu (an additional financial obligation on agri imports) is regulated in Arts 9–10.
  - Mevzuat amendment list shows amending RGs dated 6/5/2025, 1/7/2025, 29/11/2025, 31/12/2025 (No. 33124, 3. Mük.), 7/3/2026, 12/3/2026, 3/4/2026, 25/4/2026, 1/7/2026, 11/7/2026 and 27/8/2026.
  - The RG index of 27/8/2026 lists "İthalat Rejimi Kararında Değişiklik Yapılmasına İlişkin Karar (Karar Sayısı: 11644)" [VERIFIED]. Note: Karar 11648 in the same RG is a Halkbank decision, not a tariff decision; a secondary source mis-described it.

### 1c. Trade remedies

#### Law 3577 İthalatta Haksız Rekabetin Önlenmesi Hakkında Kanun (AD/CVD) — [VERIFIED]
Source: https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=3577&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5 . Adopted 14/6/1989, RG 1/7/1989 No. 20212.
- Art 4: the Directorate General of Imports (İthalat GM) may open an inquiry on complaint "veya gerektiğinde re'sen", i.e. ex officio.
- Art 6: the İthalatta Haksız Rekabeti Değerlendirme Kurulu decides whether to open or stop an investigation. It submits provisional measures and the "kesin önlem kararını Bakanlığın onayına".
- Art 12: provisional measures take the form of a guarantee (teminat), with Ministry approval and publication in the RG. "Soruşturmanın açılması tarihinden itibaren altmış gün içerisinde geçici önlem uygulanmaz. Geçici önlemlerin geçerlilik süresi dört aydır." Extendable to 6 months, or 6 and 9 months when lesser duty is examined.
- Art 13: definitive duties are set by the Kurul and approved by the Ministry. Duration, review and anti-circumvention rules are set "Cumhurbaşkanı Kararı ile".
- Art 7: retroactive application cannot exceed 90 days from the provisional measures.
- Art 15(3): the WTO ADA and SCM Agreement apply where the law is silent.
- Implementing Karar (BKK 99/13482, RG 30/10/1999 No. 23861), consolidated text on ticaret.gov.tr (https://ticaret.gov.tr/data/5b88469d13b87711604c932b/ith_haksiz_rekabet_karar.pdf), Art 7: "Kesin önlemler, ... en son gözden geçirme soruşturmasının sonuçlandığı tarihten veya yürürlüğe girdiği tarihten itibaren 5 yıl süreyle yürürlükte kalır." Expiry reviews keep measures in force while pending. Amended by 2005/9840 and CK 3113 (RG 22/10/2020).
- Regulation (Yönetmelik, RG 30/10/1999 No. 23861) Art 30: "Soruşturma, özel durumlar dışında 1 yıl içinde sonuçlandırılır. Bu süre, gerektiğinde, Kurulca 6 ayı geçmemek üzere uzatılabilir." Source: https://ticaret.gov.tr/data/5b88472713b87711604c9336/ (Yönetmelik PDF).
- The Ministry publishes a list of measures in force ("Yürürlükteki Önlemler 14.10.2026" xlsx) on its AD page (https://ticaret.gov.tr/ithalat/ticaret-politikasi-savunma-araclari/dampinge-ve-subvansiyon/damping-ve-subvansiyon-ulusal-mevzuat).

#### Safeguards — [VERIFIED]
- İthalatta Korunma Önlemleri Hakkında Karar, BKK 2004/7305 of 10/5/2004. Consolidated text: https://ticaret.gov.tr/data/5b89b18613b87618846c2306/ (Karar PDF).
  - Art 4: the "İthalatta Korunma Önlemlerini Değerlendirme Kurulu" decides on opening investigations, provisional measures and definitive measures (type, size, duration). Per Art 4(4)(f), as amended, it proposes a Presidential decision where one is needed.
  - Arts 5–6: measures may be a duty increase, an additional financial obligation, quantity or value limits, or a TRQ.
  - Art 8: decisions are published in the RG.
- Amended by CB Kararı 6729 (RG 26/1/2023 No. 32085; https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/20.5.6729.pdf, page image read). It updated the Kurul composition: chaired by the DG Imports, with members from the Ministry of Industry and Technology, the Strateji ve Bütçe Başkanlığı, TOBB, and the Ministry's DGs for Customs, Exports, International Agreements and EU. Its legal basis is the same as the İGV decision (1567/1, 474/2, 3283/2, 4458/16, 22, 55, and 2976).
- İthalatta Korunma Önlemleri Yönetmeliği (RG 8/6/2004 No. 25486; ticaret.gov.tr consolidated PDF):
  - Art 4: comment period of 40 days. "Soruşturma, Genel Müdürlükçe yürütülerek dokuz ay içinde tamamlanır. Gerekli hallerde bu süre altı ay uzatılabilir."
  - Art 11: "Korunma önleminin süresi ... geçici önlemler de dahil 4 (dört) yılı aşamaz"; liberalisation is required if the measure lasts more than one year; mid-term review if more than three years. Extension is allowed, but "önlemin toplam süresi Dünya Ticaret Örgütü üyesi ülkeler için on yılı aşamaz."
  - Re-application limits mirror WTO SG Agreement Art 7.5–7.6.
  - The 200-day cap on provisional measures is not written into the consolidated Yönetmelik text read; it follows from WTO SG Art 6 [PARTIAL].
- Recent practice: safeguard extensions of 3 years by Tebliğ (e.g. 2024/9 polyester; 2025 tebliğ on 7325.91/7326.11) [PARTIAL: lexpera/orgtr listings]. A PET-resin safeguard of USD 120/t from 19/7/2026 is reported [PARTIAL].

### 1d. Domestic indirect taxes

#### ÖTV Law 4760, Art 12 — [VERIFIED]
Source: https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=4760&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5
- Art 12(1): tax is levied at the amounts and rates in the annexed lists.
- Art 12(1) as amended by 7590/14 (Law of 24/7/2026, in force 31/7/2026): new minimum fixed ÖTV on 87.03 cars of 100,000 TL (L-class 30,000 TL), indexed annually.
- Art 12(2): the President may:
  - List (I) (fuels): raise fixed amounts "en yüksek vergi tutarının beş katına kadar artırmaya, sıfıra kadar indirmeye", and differentiate by "kullanım yerleri veya ithalatın şekline göre".
  - List (III) (tobacco/alcohol): cut to zero; raise (A) rates up to 4x and (B) rates up to 1.5x; raise minimum specific tax 2x (A) or 3x (B).
  - List (II) (vehicles), Art 12(2)(c): raise rates and the brackets of the tax base "üç katına kadar artırmaya, sıfıra kadar indirmeye", and set different rates by "motor gücü, motor silindir hacmi, çekiş sistemi, menzili, batarya kapasitesi, ... emisyon türü ve değeri".
  - New (ç), added by 7590: raise the minimum fixed amounts for 87.03 "on katına kadar", or cut to zero.
  - List (IV): cut to zero; raise to 50% (for 8517.12.00.00.11) or 25% (others).
- Note: the differentiation criteria are technical (power, range, battery, emissions), not origin. Origin-based measures therefore run through the tariff and İGV route (474/2976), not ÖTV.

#### KDV Law 3065, Art 28 — [VERIFIED]
Source: https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=3065&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5
- "Katma değer vergisi oranı, vergiye tabi her bir işlem için % 10'dur. Cumhurbaşkanı bu oranı, dört katına kadar artırmaya, % 1 'e kadar indirmeye, bu oranlar dahilinde muhtelif mal ve hizmetler ... için farklı vergi oranları tespit etmeye yetkilidir." So the statutory band is 1–40%. KDV applies equally to imports and domestic supplies (national treatment).

### 1e. Public procurement

#### Law 4734 Kamu İhale Kanunu, Art 63 — [VERIFIED]
Source: https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=4734&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5 . Art 63 was rewritten by 6518/51 (6/2/2014) and amended by 7033/74 (18/6/2017).
- (a) Below the Law 4734 Art 8 "eşik değer" thresholds, tenders may be restricted to domestic bidders.
- (b) Services and works: price advantage of up to 15% for domestic bidders.
- (c) Goods: "yerli malı teklif eden istekliler lehine, %15 oranına kadar fiyat avantajı sağlanabilir." The 15% is mandatory for listed medium/high-tech products and for domestic software.
- (d) In works, domestic-goods requirements may be imposed and are mandatory for listed machinery, materials and equipment.
- Domestic goods are certified with a "yerli malı belgesi".

#### WTO GPA status — [VERIFIED]
- WTO GPA "Parties and observers" page (https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/gproc_e/memobs_e.htm) lists "Türkiye — 4 June 1996" among observers. Türkiye is not marked "*" (pending accession negotiations), so it is an observer only and has no GPA market-access obligations.
- Constraints on procurement preferences come instead from FTA procurement provisions. Türkiye–Singapore contains "detaylı düzenleme" on public procurement [VERIFIED, Ministry page]. The Türkiye–UK FTA is a goods-only FTA (Art 1.1) [VERIFIED]. Others need case-by-case checking [PARTIAL].
- OVP 2027–29 signals more use of procurement as an industrial tool: "Kamu alımları stratejik bir politika aracı olarak daha etkin kullanılacak" [VERIFIED].

If a contracting party so elects, other contracting parties shall have the right, during the same period, to modify or withdraw, in accordance with the same procedures, concessions initially negotiated with that contracting party."   - The current triennium is 2024–26, so the deadline to reserve for 2027–29 is 31/12/2026.

## Synthesis: findings most relevant to "quiet, lawful pre-exit preparation" and "switch-on speed"
1. File a GATT Art XXVIII:5 reservation for 2027–2029 before 31/12/2026.
   - GATT XXVIII:5: "before the end of any period ... a contracting party may elect ... to reserve the right, for the duration of the next period, to modify the appropriate Schedule" [VERIFIED].
   - Filing is routine: 25–38 Members per triennium. The EU (G/MA/446, 30/7/2026) and the UK (G/MA/452, 8/10/2026) have already filed for 2027–29.
   - Türkiye filed for 2012–14 (G/MA/271) and 2021–23 (G/MA/373), but not for 2015–17, 2018–20 or 2024–26. The UK used exactly this instrument ahead of Brexit (G/MA/357, Dec 2017).
   - Cost: other Members gain reciprocal rights only over concessions initially negotiated with Türkiye.
2. Most tariff and fiscal levers are already delegated to the President and can be activated overnight by RG publication:
   - customs duties (474/2, within statutory bands);
   - additional duties and financial obligations (2976/1–3 implementing Constitution 167(2); İGV Decision 3351);
   - ÖTV (4760/12) and KDV (3065/28) rates.
   - Precedent: the 31/12/2020 RG package, including Karar 3352 deleting "İngiltere" from the domestic CU implementation decree effective 1/1/2021.
3. The binding constraints are statutory caps and WTO bindings, not procedure.
   - MFN+İGV is capped at 474-schedule levels +50% (İGV Decision Art 2(3)). Larger moves need a TBMM law.
   - About 50.5% of lines are bound (non-ag 43.0%; avg bound 17.2 vs applied 13.6). Unbound lines are free; bound lines are exposed, as DS629 (China v Türkiye) shows: EV additional duties were found to exceed bindings.
4. Trade-remedy machinery is domestic and ready, but slow.
   - AD/CVD: investigation 12+6 months; provisional measures no earlier than 60 days, lasting 4–9 months; definitive measures 5 years.
   - Safeguards: investigation 9+6 months; measures up to 4 years, 10 years total.
   - Also available: the retaliation decree (Ticari Hakların Korunması) and import-surveillance decisions. OVP 2027–29 commits to accelerating trade-defence procedures.
5. Customs plumbing is already CU-independent.
   - Türkiye is a CTC/SAD Contracting Party in its own right since 1/12/2012 (NCTS), alongside the UK, Ukraine and others.
   - Revised PEM rules were ratified by Law 7556 (2025), and EU–TR agri/ECSC protocols now have a dynamic PEM reference, giving a ready origin protocol for any post-CU FTA.
   - The no-regret step is exporter origin-readiness (supplier declarations, approved exporters).
6. Vulnerable CU-dependent assets need re-papering before exit:
   - the EU–TR AEO MRA (Decision 1/2026, OJ 13/10/2026, based on CU Decision 1/95, not yet in force; 30-day termination);
   - notified-body recognition;
   - FTAs that mirror the CU. For example, UK FTA Art 1.1(2) "preserve preferential conditions ... resulting from the bilateral trading arrangements between the European Union and Turkey".
7. FTA hedge portfolio:
   - In force with 24 named partners, including the UK, UAE (1/9/2023), Ukraine (1/10/2026, tariffs from 1/1/2027), Korea (services and investment 2018), Singapore and Malaysia (upgrade 2024).
   - Negotiating: UK upgrade (5 rounds, 11 chapters closed), Canada (launched 7/7/2026), GCC, Japan, Indonesia (PTA first).
   - Pending ratification: Lebanon, Sudan, Georgia protocols.
8. Industrial policy space after exit is wider.
   - Türkiye is a GPA observer only, and KİK Art 63 already allows a 15% domestic price preference.
   - The State Aid Law 6015 is "de facto ineffective" (EC 2025), and LCRs (renewables, tobacco) are criticised by the EU.
   - WTO TRIMs/SCM disciplines remain, per DS583 (pharma localisation found GATT III:4-inconsistent; Art 25 award 2022).
9. CBAM: Climate Law 7552 Art 8(1)(ç) already authorises a Turkish SKDM, and the ETS Regulation (RG 27/8/2026) starts a pilot (>50 kt CO2e installations; scope to be set by the Karbon Piyasası Kurulu). CBAM Art 9 credits only carbon prices "effectively paid", so a free-allocation pilot does not reduce CBAM bills.
10. Export-finance capacity is significant and can be steered quietly toward non-EU markets.
    - Eximbank 2025: 26.8 bn USD credit + 27.5 bn USD insurance; H1 2026: 32 bn USD.
    - Target-country lists give +20 points of support.
    - OVP export path: 282 bn USD (2026) → 316 bn USD (2029).
