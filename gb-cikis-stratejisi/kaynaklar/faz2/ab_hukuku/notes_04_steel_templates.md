# Notes 04 - steel regime, templates, precedents (retrieved 18 Oct 2026)

## Steel
- Regulation (EU) 2026/1384 of the EP and Council of 17 June 2026 "addressing the negative trade-related effects of global overcapacity on the Union steel market and amending Regulation (EU) 2020/2170" (CELLAR title). Per CMS: Art 207(2) TFEU, outside WTO Safeguards Agreement; in force 25 Jun 2026, applies 1 Jul 2026; 30 categories; initial combined annual TRQ 18,345,922 t (band 14.4-22.2 Mt by delegated act); out-of-quota duty 50%; melt-and-pour evidence from 1 Oct 2026; EEA EFTA excluded; no fixed expiry; replaces safeguard (Reg 2019/159) expired 30 Jun 2026.
- Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1457 of 29 June 2026 - distribution of TRQs. My parse of Annex: Türkiye country-specific allocations sum ~2.86 Mt/yr = ~15.6% of 18.35 Mt - the largest single-country allocation (Korea ~2.07 Mt, India ~1.94 Mt, Viet Nam 1.10 Mt, Ukraine 1.05 Mt, UK 1.00 Mt, China 0.80 Mt). E.g., cat. 1.A hot-rolled sheets/strip: Türkiye 642,294.94 t (largest in category). Raw text: dl/s1457.txt
  CMS: https://cms.law/en/bel/legal-updates/unprecedented-eu-steel-regulation-targets-global-overcapacity

## Mercosur
- Council Decision (EU) 2026/183 of 9 Jan 2026 on signing and provisional application of the ITA; legal basis Art 91(1), 100(2), 207(4) first subpara + 218(5) TFEU; recital 7: provisional application pending entry into force between EU and one or more Signatory Mercosur States per ITA Art 23.3. EMPA = mixed (EU + MS).
- Mandate 13 Sep 1999; concluded 6 Dec 2024; Commission proposals 3 Sep 2025; signed 17 Jan 2026 (DG TRADE page; ASIL says 16 Jan); provisional application from 1 May 2026 (DG TRADE page).
- Council vote 9 Jan 2026: per Euronews (diplomats) FR, PL, AT, HU, IE against; BE abstained; IT in favour. (PARTIAL)
- Cyprus presidency rejected declaration to bar provisional application before EP consent (bilaterals.org, 8 Jan 2026).
- EP resolution 21 Jan 2026 (P10_TA(2026)0008) requesting CJEU opinion under 218(11): 334-324-11; covers EMPA and ITA; Council doc of 11 May 2026 refers to Opinion procedure 1/26.
- Regulation (EU) 2026/687 of 11 March 2026 implementing bilateral safeguard clauses (agri). Implementing Reg (EU) 2026/888 of 20 April 2026 TRQ management.
- Uruguay & Argentina ratified ~Feb 2026; Commission said 26 Feb 2026 it "will now proceed with provisional application" (ASIL Insight vol 30 issue 5).

## Chile
- Council Decision (EU) 2023/2761 (signature) legal basis Art 91(1), 100(2), 207(4) first subpara + 218(5). Signed 13 Dec 2023. EP consent 29 Feb 2024. Council Decision (EU) 2024/3016 of 18 Mar 2024 (conclusion). ITA OJ L 2024/2953. In force 1 Feb 2025.

- AFA (mixed) awaiting MS ratifications.

## Mexico
- Negotiations launched May 2016; agreement in principle Apr 2018; concluded 17 Jan 2025; Commission proposals 3 Sep 2025; Council authorised signing 11 May 2026 (press); signed MGA + iTA 22 May 2026 (DG TRADE page). iTA "will expire and be replaced by the MGA upon the MGA's full entry into force".

## Switzerland
- Council authorised signing 24 Feb 2026 (Council press release); signed 2 Mar 2026 Brussels (IP/26/505); Swiss Federal Council dispatch 13 Mar 2026; optional referendum proposed. Package includes amending protocol + institutional protocol to the 1999 MRA (conformity assessment agreement).

## Ukraine
- Decision No 3/2025 of the EU-Ukraine Association Committee in Trade Configuration of 14 Oct 2025 on reduction/elimination of customs duties pursuant to Art 29(4) AA [OJ 2025/2130]; agreement in principle 30 Jun 2025; Council authorised 13 Oct 2025; in force 29 Oct 2025; TRQ increases conditional on alignment with EU production standards (animal welfare, pesticides, vet medicines) monitored by yearly reports; safeguard clause incl. at level of one or more MS. ATMs 4 Jun 2022 - 5 Jun 2025.
- ACAA: DG TRADE page: Priority Action Plan 2025-2026 includes "starting negotiations on an ACAA". Rada adopted bill 12221 on 7 Apr 2026 (accreditation/tech regulation alignment) - most provisions enter into force with ACAA. First-phase scope: LVD, EMC, machinery.
