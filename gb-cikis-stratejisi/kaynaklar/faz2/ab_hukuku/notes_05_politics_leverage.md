## Joint Statement 3rd EU-Türkiye HLD on Trade, Brussels 13 Oct 2026 (STATEMENT/26/2135, read via presscorner API)
- Removal of barrier on import controls under TAREKS; AEO MRA implementation; Türkiye completed alignment with new EU Machinery Regulation; continuing work on export taxes on hides/skins/wet-blue leather, import surveillance regime, textile registration surveillance, alcoholic beverages; EPREL access; technical talks on CPNP access and on batch releases for pharmaceuticals; forward-looking: alignment of technical legislation, public procurement, circular economy, business mobility, digital trade, trade defence, WTO; "took stock of cooperation on anti-circumvention of EU sanctions"; e-signature mutual recognition. No mention of CU modernisation mandate.
- Bolat (press, Yeni Safak/Daily Sabah): Council has not authorised Commission to open formal CU update negotiations "because of the position of some member states"; progress "case-by-case" on e-commerce, procurement, investment, agriculture.
## Türkiye 2025 Report SWD(2025) 756 (read)
- Additional duties on large number of non-EU-origin products in free circulation; value-based import surveillance inconsistent; no mechanism to recover overpaid duties; artificial exchange rate on pharmaceuticals; non-acceptance of EU GMP certificates; local content requirements in renewable energy and tobacco; TAREKS extra documentation contrary to CU; export taxes on hides etc.
- State aid law broadly aligned but "de facto ineffective due to the indefinite suspension of the implementation"; new incentive scheme May 2025; selective project-based incentives.
- Public procurement: no progress; significant gaps.
- Sanctions: Türkiye "consistently does not align with the EU's restrictive measures against Russia" but has taken steps vs circumvention; recommendation to extend measures to prevent re-export of economically critical goods; shadow fleet risks.
- Customs: OLAF cooperation on transhipment/false origin declarations; "continue to eliminate import and export restrictions as well as additional duties on goods in free circulation".
