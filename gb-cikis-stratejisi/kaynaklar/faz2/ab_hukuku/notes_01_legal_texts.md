# Research notes 01 - primary legal texts (retrieved 18 Oct 2026)

## Ankara Agreement (OJ L 361, 31.12.1977 EN version; CELEX 21964A1229(01)) - PDF read
- Art 22(1): Association Council "shall have the power to take decisions in the cases provided for therein. Each of the Parties shall take the measures necessary to implement the decisions taken."
- Art 22(3): once transitional stage embarked on, AC "shall adopt appropriate decisions where ... attainment of an objective of this Agreement calls for joint action ... but the requisite powers are not granted in this Agreement."
- Art 23: AC = members of Governments of MS + Council + Commission on one hand, Turkish Government on the other. "The Council of Association shall act unanimously."
- Art 25: (1) disputes on application/interpretation may be submitted to AC; (2) AC may settle by decision or submit to CJEU or any other existing court or tribunal; (3) parties must comply; (4) if not settled, AC shall determine detailed rules for arbitration/other judicial procedure.
- Arts 31-33: ratification, entry into force, languages. NO termination/denunciation/duration clause (grep for terminat/denounc/withdraw/duration = no hits).
- Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:21964A1229(01)

## Additional Protocol 1970 (CELEX 21970A1123(01))
- Art 47: anti-dumping procedure via Council of Association (22-year period) + injured Party may take protective measures.
- Art 60: safeguard - (1) Turkey may take necessary protective measures if serious disturbances in a sector / external financial stability / regional difficulties; notify AC immediately. (2) Community may take or authorise MS to take protective measures. (3) preference to measures least disturbing the Association; not exceed what is strictly necessary. (4) consultations in AC.
- Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:21970A1123(01)

## Decision 1/95 (OJ L 35, 13.2.1996; CELEX 21996D0213(01)) - verbatim extracts in dl/dec1_95_selected_articles.txt and dl/dec1_95.txt
- Art 8: within 5 years Turkey incorporates Community TBT instruments; list + conditions by AC decision within 1 year (-> Decision 2/97). Art 8(4) cooperation in standardisation, accreditation, testing, certification.
- Art 9: once Turkey has put into force the instrument, trade in the product takes place "in accordance with the conditions laid down by those instruments".
- Art 10: interim - Turkey refrains from impeding placing on market of Community products attested conforming; safeguard procedure via CU Joint Committee.
- Art 13: Turkey aligns on CCT vis-a-vis non-members; Art 14(2): CUJC may never authorise Turkey to apply a tariff lower than CCT.
- Art 16: Turkey aligns progressively with Community preferential regime (autonomous + preferential agreements); origin rules identical.
- Art 34: state aid incompatibility; Art 37: AC to adopt implementing rules within 2 yrs (never adopted; GATT Subsidies Code applies meanwhile); Art 38: appropriate measures after consultation / 45 working days.
- Art 44(1): AC may review application of trade defence instruments other than safeguard; may suspend if Turkey implemented competition, State aid control & internal-market acquis. 44(2): AP Art 47 AD modalities remain in force. Art 46: a Party may subject imports from the other Party to AD/TDI measures (derogation from free movement).
- Art 48: government procurement - AC to set a date for negotiations (never concluded).
- Art 58(1)-(2): discrepancies in legislation -> CUJC; 58(2) affected Party "may itself take the necessary protection measures and notify the Customs Union Joint Committee thereof; the latter may decide whether to amend or abolish these measures."
- Art 61 (verbatim): "Without prejudice to paragraphs 1 to 3 of Article 25 of the Ankara Agreement, if the Association Council fails to settle a dispute relating to the scope or duration of protection measures taken in accordance with Article 58 (2), safeguard measures taken in accordance with Article 63 or rebalancing measures taken in accordance with Article 64, within six months of the date on which this procedure was initiated, either Party may refer the dispute to arbitration under the procedures laid down in Article 62. The arbitration award shall be binding on the Parties to the dispute."
- Art 62: 3 arbitrators; each party appoints one within 30 days; umpire by agreement or from list of seven persons "established by the Association Council"; sits in Brussels; majority.
- Art 63: "The Parties confirm that the mechanism and modalities of safeguard measures provided for in Article 60 of the Additional Protocol remain valid."
- Art 64: "1. If a safeguard or protection measure taken by a Party creates an imbalance between the rights and obligations under this Decision, the other Party may take rebalancing measures in respect of that Party. Priority shall be given to such measures as will least disturb the functioning of the Customs Union. 2. The procedures provided for in Article 63 shall apply."
- Art 65: entry into force 31 Dec 1995 - no termination/duration clause.
- Art 66: interpretation in conformity with CJEU case law where identical in substance to EC Treaty.
