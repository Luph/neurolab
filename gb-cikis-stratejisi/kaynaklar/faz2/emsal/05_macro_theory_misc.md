# Extracts: Task 4 macro/theory + misc additions -- collected 18 Oct 2026

## Macro evidence
- Furceri, Hannan, Ostry & Rose, "Macroeconomic Consequences of Tariffs", NBER WP 25402 (Dec 2018) / IMF WP 19/9 / CEPR DP13389; 151 countries 1963-2014, local projections. 1 s.d. (3.6pp) tariff increase -> output -0.4% after 5 years; labour productivity -0.9% after 5 yrs; consumption -0.4% after 4 yrs; unemployment small (marginal) increase; Gini up; RER appreciates (significant short term); "net effects of higher tariffs on the trade balance are small and insignificant; absent shifts in saving or investment, commercial policy has little effect on the trade balance". Effects larger in expansions, advanced economies, for increases. https://www.nber.org/papers/w25402.pdf (raw/macro/fhor.txt)
- Barattieri, Cacciatore & Ghironi, "Protectionism and the business cycle", JIE 129 (2021) 103417 (NBER w24353): VARs on temporary trade barriers -> supply shock: output down, inflation up, "at best a small positive effect on the trade balance"; model: contractionary also in liquidity trap and under a PEG (GDP down, inflation up, TB improves); textbook expansionary-under-peg result only without firm entry/capital or with closed capital account. Ecuador 2015-16 safeguard surcharges: TB improved, GDP growth fell with C and I. https://www.nber.org/papers/w24353.pdf (raw/macro/bcg.txt)
- Jeanne & Son, "To What Extent Are Tariffs Offset by Exchange Rates?" NBER w27654 / JIMF 2024: US tariffs explain at most ~1/5 of USD effective appreciation 2018-19 but ~2/3 of RMB effective depreciation. https://www.nber.org/papers/w27654
- Linde & Pescatori, "The macroeconomic effects of trade tariffs: revisiting the Lerner symmetry result" (IMF WP 17/151; Riksbank WP 363, 2019): Lerner symmetry (import tariff + export subsidy neutral) holds approximately; short-run deviations from pass-through and slow FX adjustment; trade wars costly. https://www.riksbank.se/globalassets/media/rapporter/working-papers/2019/no.-363-the-macroeconomic-effects-of-trade-tariffs-revisiting-the-lerner-symmetry-result.pdf
- US 2025: goods deficit ~US$1.24trn, record (Commerce, Feb 2026); goods & services deficit US$901.5bn vs 903.5bn in 2024; imports from China -30% (news: industryweek, san.com).
- Turkiye 2020 IGV: total imports -0.9% in Q3 2020 (Bagir 2021).

## Theory
- Bhagwati & Ramaswami (1963) JPE 71(1):44-50 "Domestic Distortions, Tariffs and the Theory of Optimum Subsidy": targeting principle; production subsidy dominates tariff for production objective (Econlib bio https://www.econlib.org/library/enc/bios/bhagwati.html ; follow-up Bhagwati, Ramaswami & Srinivasan 1969 JPE 77(6):1005-10 https://ideas.repec.org/a/ucp/jpolec/v77y1969i6p1005-10.html).
- Identity: ad valorem tariff t on imports of good with domestic substitute = production subsidy at rate t to domestic producers + consumption tax at rate t on all consumption (standard trade-policy equivalence).
- WTO doctrine (DS472/497 AB, adopted 11 Jan 2019): Art III:8(b) covers payments of subsidies exclusively to domestic producers (eligibility defined by activity in product market OK), but EXCLUDES exemption/reduction of internal taxes; use-of-domestic-goods requirements remain subject to III:4 / SCM 3.1(b). (one AB member dissented on "payment of subsidies").
