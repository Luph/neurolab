# Extracts: Task 1 (Russia, Indonesia, Brazil WTO) -- collected 18 Oct 2026

## Russia recycling (utilization) fee
- WTO DS462 (EU): consultations 9 Jul 2013; panel request 11 Oct 2013; deferred 22 Oct 2013; panel ESTABLISHED 25 Nov 2013 (3rd parties: China, India, Japan, Korea, Norway, Turkey, Ukraine, US; later Brazil). WTO page lists NO composition, no report. Measure: Fed. Law 89-FZ as amended by 128-FZ; Gov. Resolution 870 (30 Aug 2012). EU: fee imposed only on imports; domestic vehicles and vehicles from Belarus/Kazakhstan exempt under conditions; progressive fee, differentiates new vs >3yrs old. Claims: GATT I:1, II:1(a)(b), III:2, III:4; TRIMs 2.1, 2.2.
  Source: https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/dispu_e/cases_e/ds462_e.htm (downloaded raw/ds462.htm)
- WTO DS463 (Japan): consultations 24 Jul 2013; claims GATT I:1, II:1, III:2, III:4; TRIMs 2.1/2.2; TBT 2.1/2.2. Exemption for companies registered in Russia committing to produce in RU/BY/KZ with specific manufacturing operations. Also cites Resolution 520 (20 Jun 2013) on subsidies to reimburse waste-handling costs. No panel request on WTO page.
  Source: https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/dispu_e/cases_e/ds463_e.htm
- USTR 2022 Report on Russia's WTO Compliance (p.24): Russia amended fee "by removing the exemption for motor vehicles manufactured in the EAEU"; "Russia has consistently increased the recycling fee, most recently in 2021 by as high as 400 percent on some equipment"; "U.S. stakeholders contend that although the recycling fee appears non-discriminatory because both importers and domestic producers must pay it, in fact, treatment is not equal because Russia provides subsidies that effectively reimburse domestic producers (except foreign-owned producers)"; 2019 revision: points-based localization -> higher localization, higher compensation from federal budget.
  Source: https://ustr.gov/sites/default/files/2022-12/2022%20Report%20on%20Russia's%20WTO%20Compliance.pdf
- Automotive Logistics: from 2014 carmakers pay fee on both locally assembled and imported vehicles, but government reimburses locally made; increase tied to tariff cuts on finished vehicles after WTO accession.
- Indexation: Gov. Resolution No. 1255 of 13 Sep 2024: from 1 Oct 2024 coefficients +70-85%; then +10-20% each 1 January through 2030 (zr.ru / profile.ru / ucsol.ru). From 1 Dec 2025 fee for personal-use imports depends on engine power; preferential rate only up to 160 hp (finuslugi; Reuters).
- Fee revenue: draft 2026 budget ~1.65 trn RUB (+46.7% vs 1.12 trn 2025 forecast); only 267 bn collected by autumn 2025 (Reuters via Yahoo Finance https://finance.yahoo.com/news/russia-expects-vehicle-scrappage-fee-154337583.html). VAT 20%->22% from 1 Jan 2026.
- Import shares (Autostat): imports ~half of sales in 2009; 32.8% in 2013 (marketing.rbc.ru/autonews). 2022 imports 25% -> 2023 54% (Chinese official imports 14%->42%; local assembly of foreign brands 46%->13%) https://www.autostat.ru/news/56554/
- 2025 (Autoreview No.3 2026, https://autoreview.ru/articles/avtorynok/util-effekt): new car sales -15.3% to ~1,335k; Lada/UAZ 455k->348k; legal imports 758k->440k; parallel imports flat 156k; locally assembled foreign brands 208k->391k (29% of market); total imports -35%; growth of local assembly mostly Chinese models with near-zero local content; domestic models got more expensive at pace comparable to imports.
- Aug 2026 (Izvestia 3 Sep 2026 citing Autostat, https://iz.ru/2160661/2026-09-03/import-v-rf-moshchnykh-avto-s-probegom-dostig-rekordnogo-minimuma): local assembly 64.1% of passenger car market (Aug 2026), +11pp y/y; alternative imports 59% of new-car imports (Aug), 48.9% Jan-Aug; share of >160hp among used imports 2.3% (vs ~46% Aug 2025; >67% Oct-Nov 2025).
- EAEU: from 1 Apr 2024 Russia charges fee difference on cars customs-cleared in other EAEU states (Kyrgyz re-export channel) (timesca.com). Kazakhstan introduced own utilization fee 1 Jan 2016 (Avtovaz: +$2,000/Lada Granta) (automotivelogistics.media). Kazakhstan 2026 order to raise scrap fee on cars from Russia to remove gap/arbitrage (orda.kz). 2025: 17.1k new + 19.1k used from Belarus; 53.6k new + 2.8k used from Kyrgyzstan (Avtostat).

## Indonesia
- WTO DS54/55/59/64 Indonesia -- Autos: panel report 2 Jul 1998, adopted 23 Jul 1998: 1996 National Car Programme (luxury sales tax and customs duty exemptions for "national cars" tied to local content) inconsistent with GATT I and III:2, TRIMs Art. 2, SCM 5(c) serious prejudice (EC). Indonesia complied via 1999 Automotive Policy (24 Jun 1999). https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/dispu_e/cases_e/ds54_e.htm ; https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/dispu_e/cases_e/1pagesum_e/ds55sum_e.pdf
- LCGC (PP 41/2013): PPnBM exemption; 980-1,200cc; >=20 km/l; rising local content; ~Rp100m launch price (Gaikindo). LCGC >20% of market since 2016 (JBIC).
- 2021 PPnBM DTP: PMK 20/2021; Kepmenperin 169/2021: >=70% local purchase; 21 models/6 makers; 100% Mar-May, 50% Jun-Aug, 25% Sep-Dec (extended); PMK 31/2021 extended to 1,500-2,500cc with >=60% local purchase. Cost Rp4.63 trn (APBN Kita Jan 2022 via DDTC https://news.ddtc.co.id/ini-laporan-kemenkeu-soal-realisasi-insentif-pajak-mobil-2021-36246); sales 887,202 (2021) vs 532,407 (2020) (+67%, COVID base); BKF press release Sep 2021: retail sales Jan-Jul +38.5% y/y, production +49.4% (https://fiskal.kemenkeu.go.id/files/siaran-pers/file/1631841695_siaran_pers_perpanjangan_insentif_ppnbm_dtp.pdf). No WTO challenge found.

## Brazil Inovar-Auto: DS472 (EU) / DS497 (Japan)
- Panel report 30 Aug 2017; AB report 13 Dec 2018; adopted 11 Jan 2019. Tax (IPI) reductions only for Brazil-made vehicles -> GATT III:2; accreditation, production steps -> III:4 & TRIMs 2.1; AB upheld III:4 (foreign makers must accredit as importers/distributors -> burden); AB REVERSED SCM 3.1(b) finding on minimum manufacturing steps; MFN violation vs MERCOSUR/Mexico preferences not justified by Enabling Clause. Panel rejected Art III:8(b) defence (subsidy to domestic producers does not shield tax discrimination). Panel: non-discriminatory subsidies or lowering trade barriers would be more effective for safety/efficiency objectives (Art XX(b)/(g) defences rejected). RPT ended 31 Dec 2019; Brazil (16 Jan 2020): Inovar-Auto expired without renewal (end 2017) -> claims full compliance.
  https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/dispu_e/cases_e/ds472_e.htm ; ds497_e.htm
