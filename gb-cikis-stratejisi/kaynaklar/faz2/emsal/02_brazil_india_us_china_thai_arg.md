## Brazil Rota 2030 / MOVER
- Rota 2030 (MP 843/2018 -> Law 13,755/2018): mandatory requirements (labelling, energy efficiency, structural/safety) for vehicles produced in Brazil AND imported (HS 87.01-87.06); MP text: imported goods must get treatment "não menos favorável" than domestic; penalty up to 20% of revenue/customs value. Habilitation open to firms that produce in Brazil OR that only commercialize (importers). IPI cut up to 2pp (efficiency) and 1pp (safety) for vehicles "fabricados no Brasil ou importados" from 2022. R&D: IRPJ/CSLL deduction for habilitated firms. Sources: https://legis.senado.leg.br/norma/27431688/publicacao/27431912 ; https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/lei/2018/lei-13755-10-dezembro-2018-787430-normaatualizada-pl.html
- MOVER (Law 14,902 of 27 Jun 2024) replaced Rota 2030 (ICCT Oct 2025, https://theicct.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/ID-412-%E2%80%93-MOVER-program_policy-update_final.pdf). Mandatory efficiency/CO2 (well-to-wheel) targets for manufacturers AND importers (Decree 12,435/2025). "Green IPI" Decree 12,549 (Jul 2025): IPI varies by propulsion, fuel, power, structural safety, ADAS, recyclability; e.g. BEV -2pp, gasoline +6.5pp vs 6.3% base. BUT: full IPI EXEMPTION ("IPI zero") only for small cars <=83 g CO2/km WtW, >=80% recyclable AND "manufactured in Brazil, including stamping external panels, welding, painting, engine manufacturing, and assembly" -> origin/production-step condition re-introduced (Inovar-Auto-type III:2 risk). Valid to 31 Dec 2026. Financial credits for R&D/production ~R$19.3bn 2024-2028 (InfluenceMap/PwC). Special import rate for auto parts without national production (PwC).
- EV import tariffs re-instated (Gecex Nov 2023): BEV 10% (Jan 2024) -> 18% (Jul 2024) -> 25% (Jul 2025) -> 35% (Jul 2026); hybrids 12%->25%->30%->35%; PHEV 12%->20%->28%->35%; duty-free quotas declining to 2027 (autoindustria.com.br; newsquawk). BYD Camaçari plant.

## India PLI
- 14 PLI schemes; outlay ~Rs1.97 lakh crore (later Rs1.91 lakh crore). As of 30 Jun 2026 (DPIIT via angelone/gktoday/dailypioneer): disbursed Rs36,754 crore since 2020 (Rs19,091 crore to large-scale electronics); investment Rs2.59 lakh crore; production+sales Rs23.79 lakh crore; exports Rs15.53 lakh crore; 14.57 lakh jobs. Disbursement < 1/5 of outlay. Mobile phones: exports Rs22,870 cr (FY21) -> Rs2,00,000 cr (FY25); production +146% to Rs5.25 lakh cr; imports -77%; >99% of domestic demand met locally (govt claims). https://www.angelone.in/news/economy/pli-incentives-total-36-754-crore-since-2020-with-1-400-crore-disbursed-in-q1-fy27
- WTO: DS642 (China v India): PLI ACC battery, PLI Auto, EV Passenger Cars Scheme (SPMEPCI); consultations 15 Oct 2025; claims SCM 3.1(b)/3.2, GATT III:4, TRIMs 2.1, GATT I:1 (EV scheme); panel established 24 Feb 2026; composed 28 May 2026. https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/dispu_e/cases_e/ds642_e.htm . DVA thresholds reported: auto PLI 50% DVA, ACC 25% (secondary: visionias). DS644 (China v India, 19 Dec 2025): ICT tariffs (II:1) + Solar Module Programme (III:4, TRIMs, SCM 3.1(b)); panel established 23 Jun 2026. https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/dispu_e/cases_e/ds644_e.htm

## China NEV purchase tax
- 2024-2025 full exemption up to RMB30,000/car; 2026-2027 halved (5% effective rate), cap RMB15,000; PHEV range threshold 43km->100km from 2026; MOF est. total RMB520bn for 2024-27 (Xu Hongcai); 2022: 5.7m EVs exempted, RMB88bn. Imported NEV models can apply for the catalogue (need special inspection report). Li-ion battery consumption tax 2% from 1 Sep 2026, 4% Sep 2027. Vehicle & vessel tax benefits for PHEV/EREV/commercial BEV end 1 Jan 2027. (electrive Jul 2026 https://www.electrive.com/2026/07/china-further-reduces-tax-incentives-for-evs/ ; fastmarkets https://fastmarkets.com/insights/china-extends-nev-purchase-tax-breaks-to-2027)

## Thailand EV 3.0 / 3.5
- EV 3.5 (2024-2027): subsidies 50,000-100,000 baht (battery-size dependent) for cars < 2m baht; excise 8%->2% for EVs up to 7m baht; import duty/excise privileges 2024-2025 conditional on local production 2:1 by 2026, 3:1 by 2027; subsidy repayment if shortfall (EY https://globaltaxnews.ey.com/news/2024-0356). EV 3.0 (2022-23): up to 150,000 baht/unit; ratio originally 1:1 (2024) or 1:1.5 (2025), later rolled into 1:2/1:3 with reduced incentives (carz.com.my Dec 2024). Dec 2025 Cabinet: export production counts 1.5x toward compensation; deadlines extended; subsidy suspended if conditions not met; imported battery cells limited to 10% of factory price from 1 Jan 2026 (nationthailand https://www.nationthailand.com/business/automobile/40059499). Excise to rise 2%->10% in 2026 for EV3.0 (?). EV share 18% of sales 2025. 10 Sep 2026: new excise structure tied to local manufacturing/components approved in principle (ASEAN Briefing 17 Sep 2026).

## Argentina
