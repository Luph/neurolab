# Extracts: Task 3 pass-through -- collected 18 Oct 2026

## US 2018-19 tariffs
- Amiti, Redding & Weinstein (2019) JEP 33(4):187-210 (CEPR DP13564 version): "complete passthrough of the tariffs into domestic prices of imported goods"; "full incidence of the tariff falls on domestic consumers"; by end-2018 "$3 billion per month in added tax costs and another $1.4 billion per month in deadweight" loss. https://www.aeaweb.org/doi/10.1257/jep.33.4.187 ; https://www.princeton.edu/~reddings/papers/CEPR-DP13564.pdf
- Fajgelbaum, Goldberg, Kennedy & Khandelwal (2020) QJE 135(1):1-55: targeted import prices (ex-duty) did not fall -> complete pass-through to duty-inclusive prices; loss to US consumers/firms buying imports $51bn (0.27% GDP); net aggregate real income loss $7.2bn (0.04% GDP) after tariff revenue and producer gains. https://ideas.repec.org/p/nbr/nberwo/25638.html
- Cavallo, Gopinath, Neiman & Tang (2021) AER: Insights 3(1):19-34: 20% tariff -> ex-tariff price -1.1%, importer total price +18.9% (~95% PT); alt. 10% tariff -> -0.6% ex-tariff, +9.4% total; ERPT 22% in 12 months (much lower than tariff PT); US exporters cut ex-tariff prices ~33% of retaliatory tariff after 12m (~45% for exports to China); retail: 20% tariff -> +0.7% relative retail price at two large retailers (margins compressed), washing machines exception (high, rapid PT). https://web.ntpu.edu.tw/~guan/courses/Cavallo-etal21.pdf ; NBER w26396

## Turkiye: IGV (additional customs duties)
- Bagir (2021) CBRT WP 21/13 "Tariff Changes by Turkey During the Covid-19 Pandemic: Impact on Import Value and Import Prices": 6 Presidential decrees (18 Apr, 21 Apr x2, 11 May, 21 May, 28 Jun 2020) -> 5,088 of 16,437 GTIP lines (1,912 new IGV; 3,174 raised) temporary to Oct 2020 (extended to Dec 2020 by 24 Sep decree). Only 6.5% of imports affected (EU, EFTA, FTA partners exempt); weighted mean increase on affected pairs 11.32pp; overall burden on total imports 0.73pp; 1pp tariff -> -1.24% import value; total imports -0.9% in Q3 2020; NO significant effect on import (border) prices -> cost "entirely borne by Turkish firms and consumers" (Turkey price-taker). https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/d79e967a-0fac-4092-8d66-f944673ae591/wp2113.pdf?MOD=AJPERES (raw/tcmb/wp2113.txt)
- TEPAV note: 20 May 2020 IGV on 859 products, US$2.32bn imports, ~US$161m/yr revenue. https://tepav.org.tr/en/haberler/s/10144
- 2018 retaliatory duties on US-origin goods (from 11 Jun 2018; doubled Aug 2018) abolished 21 Sep 2025 by Decree No. 10435 (Mondaq).
- TCMB IR 2017-II: Q1 2017 clothing and other core goods prices rose strongly "gerek kur etkileri gerekse bazı ürünlerdeki gümrük vergisi artışları neticesinde".
- 2011 apparel IGV: industry claims only (no CPI quantification found).

## Turkiye: OTV/KDV episodes (TCMB)
- 2009: car OTV (<=1600cc) 37%->18% (15 Mar-15 Jun 2009), 27% to 30 Sep 2009; white goods OTV 6.7%->0 then 2%; KDV furniture & IT 18->8 (Apr 2009, 3 months) (Maliye press release via alomaliye). TCMB IR 2009-II: Q1 2009 durables (ex-gold) prices -2.49%, annual -0.95%, car prices main driver. https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/f2a0cee1-bc41-4cd1-b77c-a5ccaec010e3/enf-nisan2009.pdf?MOD=AJPERES
- 2017 (not 2016): BKK 2017/9759 (3 Feb 2017): furniture KDV 18->8, white goods OTV 6.7->0 to 30 Apr 2017, extended to end-Sep 2017. TCMB IR 2017-II: pass-through "relatively limited" due to TL depreciation; furniture prices -2.54% in Q1 2017. https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/8c32d9d1-cc4a-428e-824c-ab1a79625a21/enf-nisan2017_tam.pdf?MOD=AJPERES
- Nov 2018 package (31 Oct 2018): white goods OTV 6.7->0, furniture KDV 18->8, car OTV -15pp (<=1600cc), commercial vehicles KDV 18->1, housing KDV 8% extended; extended in 2019, expired end-June 2019. TCMB Fiyat Gelismeleri Kasim 2018: CPI -1.44% m/m; core goods annual -7.46pp; cars -13.59%, furniture -8.50%, white goods -5.23% in Nov -> "vergi indirimlerinin fiyatlara büyük oranda yansıtıldığına işaret etmiştir". https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/428f5a92-656d-4043-98b2-e6627b1b035a/afiyatkasim18.pdf?MOD=AJPERES ; IR 2019-I: Q4 2018 durables -10.69%. AXA bulletin cites estimated temporary -0.9pp CPI effect (source of estimate unclear). IR 2019-III: expiry end-June 2019 -> temporary rise in July annual inflation.
  My calc of implied pass-through: white goods 1/1.067-1 = -6.3% vs -5.23% observed (~83%); furniture 1.08/1.18-1 = -8.5% vs -8.50% (~100%).
- TCMB IR 2017-II Box 3.1 historical decomposition (Table 2): tax adjustments' contribution to CPI avg 0.8pp/yr 2006-2016; 1.8pp (2010), 1.4pp (2016), 1.1 (2011), 0.5 (2009). Exchange rate contribution avg 1.3pp, 4.0pp in 2015.
- TCMB IR 2018-III Box 3.1 tobacco: retail-price-based OTV creates fiscal multiplier ~8 (10 kurus producer/specific-tax rise -> ~80 kurus retail), cut to 6.8 after July 2018 reform (ad valorem 65.25%->63%, specific 32.46->42 kurus).

## Turkiye ERPT / second round
- CBRT blog (Kara, Ogunc, Ozmen, Sarikaya, ~2017): cumulative 1-yr ERPT ~15% (Kara & Ogunc 2012), ~25% in overheating, <10% in downturns; higher when strong depreciation expected; ER contribution 1.1pp/yr avg 2006-2015, 1.8pp 2011-2015; pre-float indexation made services sensitive to FX. https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/blog/en/main+menu/analyses/exchange+rate+pass-through
- TCMB IR 2017-II Box 3.1: ER pass-through ~13% short run, up to 18% long run incl. inertia; import price PT ~9% (13% LR) end-2016 vs ~12% (17%) earlier; output gap +1pp for a year -> +0.5pp inflation LR.
- TCMB IR 2023-III (27 Jul 2023): cost-channel ERPT ~25% over 1 year (10% basket depreciation -> ~2.5pp CPI); pass-through rose after 2018 and accelerated from 2020; drivers: dollarization, CA deficit, FX debt, risk premium, higher durable-goods weight (~2x 2005); highest in core goods (autos) and energy; expectations channel affects rent, education, health. (Bloomberg HT https://www.bloomberght.com/tcmb-kurun-enflasyona-etkisini-hesapladi-2335510)
- Kara & Ogunc (2008) EMFT 44(6):52-66: pass-through weaker & slower after IT adoption.
